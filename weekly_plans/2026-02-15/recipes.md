#### Winter Citrus & Radicchio Salad *(Composed)*

**Cuisine:** Italian
A stunning composed salad of blood orange and cara cara segments arranged with shaved radicchio, creamy burrata, toasted pistachios, and Castelvetrano olives. A visual and flavor masterpiece.

**Key Ingredients:** blood oranges, cara cara oranges, radicchio, burrata, pistachios, Castelvetrano olives

**Dressing:** Blood orange vinaigrette: fresh blood orange juice, champagne vinegar, Dijon mustard, extra-virgin olive oil, minced shallot, flaky salt, cracked black pepper


**Why these flavors work:** The bitter radicchio is tamed by sweet citrus segments and creamy burrata, while the acid in the vinaigrette brightens every bite. Pistachios add crunch and richness. This follows the Italian principle of balancing amaro (bitter) with dolce (sweet) and grasso (fat).


🌿 *Blood oranges and radicchio are at their peak on the East Coast in January–March.*

**Source:** [Food52](https://www.food52.com/recipes/winter-citrus-radicchio-salad)
*Dinner-sized composed salad with stunning winter citrus at peak season.*


#### Warm Farro & Roasted Root Vegetable Salad *(Tossed)*

**Cuisine:** Mediterranean
Chewy pearled farro tossed with roasted beets, carrots, and parsnips, crumbled goat cheese, toasted hazelnuts, and a warm sherry vinaigrette. Hearty, warming, and substantial.

**Key Ingredients:** pearled farro, beets, carrots, parsnips, goat cheese, hazelnuts, shallot

**Dressing:** Warm sherry vinaigrette: sherry vinegar, Dijon mustard, minced shallot, extra-virgin olive oil, honey, fresh thyme, salt, black pepper. Whisked warm to slightly wilt the greens.


**Why these flavors work:** Earthy roasted roots provide sweetness and depth, while the nutty farro adds chew and substance. The tangy goat cheese and bright vinaigrette cut through the richness, and hazelnuts provide crunch contrast. The warm dressing ties everything together.


🌿 *Root vegetables (beets, carrots, parsnips) and farro are winter staples on the East Coast.*

**Source:** [Epicurious](https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/warm-farro-salad)
*Tossed dinner salad with winter roots at peak season. Hearty enough to stand alone.*

#### Roasted Beet & Pomegranate Salad with Labneh
**Cuisine:** Middle Eastern
Roasted golden and red beets with jewel-toned pomegranate seeds, creamy labneh, toasted walnuts, and fresh mint. A stunning, flavor-packed side.

**Key Ingredients:** golden beets, red beets, pomegranate seeds, labneh, walnuts, mint
