## Sunday - Leftover Sunday Gravy Rigatoni with Meatballs & Sausage

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Coley Cooks](https://coleycooks.com/authentic-italian-sunday-gravy/) |
| **Servings** | 4 to 6 |
| **Prep** | 10 minutes |
| **Cook** | 15 minutes with prepared gravy |

### Nonna Says

> Boil the rigatoni in water salty enough to make it pay attention. Warm the Sunday gravy low and slow, with the meatballs and sausage tucked in like family on the couch. Scoop the pasta out a minute early and finish it right in the sauce with a splash of pasta water. Toss until every ridge is red and glossy, then grate pecorino like you mean it. Do not drown it, honey, this is pasta, not a swimming lesson. Serve the meat on the side or on top and take the compliment.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → warm gently and loosen to glossy sauce**

- 3 to 4 cups leftover Sunday gravy *(with meatballs and sausage)*

- 1/2 cup reserved pasta water *(use as needed)*


**Group B → cook in salted boiling water**

- 1 pound rigatoni *(boiled 1 minute shy of al dente)*

- 1 tablespoon kosher salt *(for pasta water)*


**Group C → finish and garnish**

- 1/2 cup pecorino Romano *(freshly grated)*

- 2 tablespoons Italian parsley *(chopped, optional)*


**Final assembly:** transfer pasta to gravy → add pasta water 2 tablespoons at a time → toss 1 to 2 minutes until coated → serve with meatballs, sausage, pecorino, and parsley

### Variations

1. **Pot Roast Sunday Sauce Rigatoni** - Swap in chuck roast for some of the pork so the sauce gains a deeper beefy backbone and ragù-style texture. ([recipe](https://linacooks.com/recipes/sunday-sauce-rigatoni))

2. **Red Wine Sunday Gravy** - Deglaze the pot with dry red wine before the tomatoes for a darker, more aromatic sauce. ([recipe](https://dinoromano.com/2025/08/07/hearty-italian-sunday-gravy-thatll-win-nonnas-approval/))

3. **Sausage and Meatball Sunday Gravy** - Keep the gravy focused on sausage and meatballs, then simmer cheese rinds in the sauce for extra savory depth. ([recipe](https://www.food.com/recipe/sunday-gravy-with-sausages-and-meatballs-496273))

## Monday - Wagyu Ultra-Smashed Cheeseburgers with Pickles & Secret Sauce

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/ultra-smashed-cheeseburger-recipe-food-lab) |
| **Servings** | 3 double cheeseburgers |
| **Prep** | 15 minutes |
| **Cook** | 10 minutes |

### Nonna Says

> Heat the griddle until it scares you a little, then keep your buns and sauce ready. Drop loose wagyu balls on the metal and smash hard once, because pressing twice is how you make sadness. Salt, pepper, flip when the edges go lacy and brown, and blanket one patty with American cheese. Stack the second patty on top, slide it onto the sauced bun, and add pickles and lettuce fast. Serve immediately while the edges still crackle. Look at that, dinner in ten minutes and nobody had to sulk.

### How It Comes Together

*Preheat oven to cast-iron griddle over high heat until lightly smoking*

**Group A → smash on hot griddle and season**

- 12 ounces ground wagyu beef *(divided into six loose 2-ounce balls)*

- 1 teaspoon kosher salt *(for seasoning patties)*

- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper *(freshly ground)*


**Group B → build chilled bun station**

- 3 potato buns *(split and toasted)*

- 3 tablespoons secret sauce *(mixed ahead)*

- 3/4 cup shredded lettuce *(washed and dried)*

- 18 slices dill pickles *(drained)*


**Group C → melt cheese during final flip**

- 6 slices American cheese *(ready by griddle)*

- 1 tablespoon neutral oil *(only if griddle is not seasoned)*


**Final assembly:** place beef balls on griddle → smash very thin for 10 seconds → cook 45 to 60 seconds until edges crisp → scrape, flip, cheese one patty, stack second patty → transfer to dressed buns and serve immediately

### Variations

1. **Onion Smashed Burger** - Smash thinly sliced onions under each beef ball so they fuse into a sweet, browned onion crust. ([recipe](https://arteflame.com/blogs/recipes/ultimate-smashed-cheeseburger-recipe))

2. **Umami Bomb Smash Burger** - Season the beef or sauce with Worcestershire, fish sauce, and smoked paprika for a deeper savory punch. ([recipe](https://basilandbubbly.com/the-ultimate-smash-burger-recipe-for-crispy-juicy-perfection/))

3. **Food Lab Double Smash Burger** - Keep the ultra-thin double-stack technique but vary the toppings with griddled onions, mustard, or extra cheese. ([recipe](https://food52.com/recipes/55658-the-food-lab-s-ultra-smashed-cheeseburgers))

## Tuesday - Alex's Mom's Stuffed Cabbage Rolls

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Smitten Kitchen](https://smittenkitchen.com/2009/02/alexs-moms-stuffed-cabbage/) |
| **Servings** | 4 to 6 |
| **Prep** | 35 minutes |
| **Cook** | 45 minutes |

### Nonna Says

> Soften the cabbage leaves first, because nobody can roll a stiff blanket. Cook the onion, carrot, celery, and parsnip until sweet, then mix them with beef, rice, tomato paste, salt, and pepper. Roll each leaf snug, seam side down, and give them enough tomato sauce to lounge in. Add a little brown sugar and vinegar if the sauce needs that sweet-sour hug. Simmer covered until the rice is tender and the cabbage gives up. Spoon extra sauce over the top and be proud, these are patient food.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → separate flexible leaves and trim thick ribs**

- 1 head Savoy cabbage *(cored, soaked in boiling water 10 minutes, leaves dried)*


**Group B → sauté until soft, then cool**

- 2 tablespoons olive oil

- 1 small to medium onion *(finely chopped)*

- 1 carrot *(shredded)*

- 1 stalk celery *(thinly sliced)*

- 1 parsnip *(shredded)*


**Group C → mix with cooled vegetables for filling**

- 1 pound ground beef

- 1/2 cup uncooked white rice *(rinsed)*

- 1 to 2 tablespoons tomato paste

- 1 teaspoon kosher salt *(plus more to taste)*

- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper


**Group 4 → stir sauce together**

- 3 to 4 cups simple tomato sauce, tomato juice, or V8

- 1 tablespoon brown sugar *(optional, for sweet balance)*

- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar *(optional, for tang)*


**Final assembly:** fill each leaf with 1/4 to 1/3 cup filling → roll tightly and place seam side down → cover with sauce → bring to boil, then simmer covered 45 minutes → serve with extra warm sauce

### Variations

1. **Sweet-and-Sour Stuffed Cabbage** - Add brown sugar and vinegar to the tomato sauce for a classic Jewish-style sweet-and-sour balance. ([recipe](https://www.savoryexperiments.com/cabbage-rolls-with-tomato-sauce-recipe/))

2. **Cheesy Stuffed Cabbage Rolls** - Tuck shredded mozzarella or cheddar into the filling and melt more over the top before serving. ([recipe](https://homechefworld.com/stuffed-cabbage-rolls-with-tomato-sauce/))

3. **Pork-and-Beef Golabki** - Replace half the beef with ground pork for a richer, juicier Eastern European filling. ([recipe](https://amiraspantry.com/stuffed-cabbage-rolls-recipe/))

## Wednesday - Slow-Cooked Pork Chops with Mushroom Cream Sauce

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [The Modern Proper](https://themodernproper.com/pork-chops-with-mushroom-cream-sauce) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 minutes |
| **Cook** | 3 1/2 to 4 hours |

### Nonna Says

> Season the pork chops like you have confidence, then brown them before they go anywhere near the slow cooker. Pile in the mushrooms, garlic, thyme, wine, and a little broth, and let everything cook low until the chops turn tender. Do not blast them on high unless you enjoy chewing furniture. Whisk in cream and Dijon near the end so the sauce stays silky, not broken. Finish with lemon and chives, then spoon mushrooms over every chop. Serve it with something soft for catching sauce.

### How It Comes Together

*Preheat oven to slow cooker on LOW*

**Group A → season and sear 2 to 3 minutes per side**

- 4 1 1/2-inch bone-in pork chops *(patted dry)*

- 1 teaspoon kosher salt

- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper *(freshly cracked)*

- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil *(for browning)*


**Group B → sauté mushrooms and aromatics 6 minutes**

- 6 tablespoons salted butter *(divided)*

- 2 pounds cremini mushrooms *(trimmed and halved)*

- 3 cloves garlic *(minced)*

- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme *(minced)*


**Group C → deglaze skillet and pour into slow cooker**

- 1 cup dry white wine

- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth *(for slow cooker moisture)*


**Group 4 → whisk cream finish into hot juices**

- 1 cup heavy cream

- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard

- 1 lemon *(halved)*

- 2 tablespoons fresh chives *(chopped)*


**Final assembly:** place chops over mushroom mixture in slow cooker → cook LOW 3 to 4 hours until tender and about 145°F to 150°F → remove chops and tent loosely → whisk cream and Dijon into sauce and cook 15 to 20 minutes → finish with lemon and chives

### Variations

1. **Garlic Parmesan Mushroom Pork Chops** - Stir Parmesan into the mushroom cream sauce for a saltier, cheesier finish. ([recipe](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/pork-chops-with-mushroom-cream-sauce/))

2. **Dijon Mushroom Cream Pork Chops** - Lean harder into Dijon mustard for tang that cuts through the cream and pork richness. ([recipe](https://therecipecritic.com/pork-chops-mushroom-cream-sauce/))

3. **Bacon Mushroom Cream Pork Chops** - Render bacon first and build the mushroom sauce in the smoky drippings. ([recipe](https://www.saltandlavender.com/pork-chops-with-mushroom-cream-sauce/))

## Thursday - Greek-Style Roasted Branzino with Garlicky Kale

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/roasted-branzino-recipe/) |
| **Servings** | 3 to 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 minutes |
| **Cook** | 20 minutes |

### Nonna Says

> Dry the branzino well and cut slits in the sides so the seasoning has somewhere to go. Stuff the bellies with lemon, onion, and dill, then roast hot until the flesh flakes and the skin blisters. Whisk the ladolemono until it looks glossy and bossy, then pour it over the fish while it is still sizzling. Keep the kale moving in a garlicky pan until it turns bright and tender. Do not overcook the fish, honey, it is not a punishment. Finish with tomatoes, herbs, and one more squeeze of lemon.

### How It Comes Together

*Preheat oven to 400°F, center rack, broiler ready for finish*

**Group A → slash, oil, and season fish**

- 2 whole branzino, 1 to 1 1/2 pounds each *(cleaned, scaled, patted dry)*

- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt *(divided)*

- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil *(for sheet pan and fish)*


**Group B → stuff fish cavities**

- 1 lemon *(half sliced for stuffing, half juiced)*

- 1/2 red onion *(thinly sliced)*

- 1/4 cup fresh dill *(chopped, plus stems for cavity)*


**Group C → whisk ladolemono until emulsified**

- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice

- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil

- 1 clove garlic *(grated)*

- 1 teaspoon dried oregano


**Group 4 → salt lightly for topping**

- 1 cup cherry tomatoes *(halved)*

- 1/4 cup fresh parsley *(chopped)*


**Final assembly:** roast fish 5 minutes on first side → turn carefully and roast 5 to 7 minutes more to 145°F → broil 3 to 4 minutes until skin chars → move to platter and spoon on ladolemono → top with tomatoes, dill, and parsley

*Meanwhile, cook the kale:*

**sauté kale over medium-high heat**

- 1 large bunch kale *(stemmed, chopped, washed)*

- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil


**add garlic, season, and brighten**

- 3 cloves garlic *(thinly sliced)*

- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt *(plus more to taste)*

- 1 tablespoon lemon juice


**Then:** cook 4 to 6 minutes until bright and tender → finish with lemon juice → serve beside roasted fish

### Variations

1. **Greek Branzino with Ladolemono** - Double down on lemon, garlic, oregano, and olive oil for a sharper Greek finish. ([recipe](https://recipesbyclare.com/recipes/greek-roasted-branzino))

2. **Branzino with Potatoes, Tomatoes, and Feta** - Roast the fish over a bed of potatoes, tomatoes, and onion, then finish with feta for a fuller one-pan meal. ([recipe](https://makeitmediterranean.com/greek-style-roasted-branzino/))

3. **Whole Roasted Branzino with Lemon and Herbs** - Use rosemary, parsley, and lemon for a simpler herb-roasted version with a classic Mediterranean profile. ([recipe](https://www.foodandwine.com/recipes/whole-roasted-branzino-with-lemon-and-rosemary))

## Friday - Babo Pasta: Egg-Yolk Spinach Tagliatelle with Brown Butter & Parm

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/fresh-spinach-pasta-dough-recipe) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 1 hour 15 minutes including rest |
| **Cook** | 5 minutes |

### Nonna Says

> Cook the spinach dry, squeeze it harder than your inbox squeezes you, and chop it fine. Pile the flour and semolina, drop in the yolks and spinach, and knead until the dough feels smooth and alive. Rest it, roll it thin, and cut tagliatelle like ribbons for a party. Brown the butter slowly until it smells nutty, not burnt, and watch closely. Boil the pasta for just a couple minutes, then toss it with butter, Parm, and pasta water until shiny. Serve right away and let Babo take the credit.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → stir into green yolk mixture**

- 6 ounces fresh spinach *(wilted, squeezed very dry, finely chopped)*

- 8 large egg yolks *(room temperature)*

- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil


**Group B → mound and make well**

- 2 cups 00 flour *(plus more for dusting)*

- 1/2 cup semolina flour

- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt


**Group C → adjust hydration while kneading**

- 1 to 3 tablespoons water *(only if dough feels dry)*


**Final assembly:** fork yolk mixture into flour → knead 8 to 10 minutes until smooth → wrap and rest 30 minutes → roll through pasta machine and cut tagliatelle → boil in salted water 2 to 3 minutes

*Meanwhile, make the brown butter sauce:*

**brown in skillet until nutty**

- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter *(cut into pieces)*


**emulsify with hot pasta**

- 1 cup Parmigiano-Reggiano *(finely grated)*

- 1/2 cup pasta water *(reserved)*

- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper *(freshly ground)*


**Then:** add drained tagliatelle to brown butter → toss with cheese and pasta water → serve glossy and hot

### Variations

1. **Lemon Pine Nut Spinach Tagliatelle** - Finish the brown butter pasta with lemon zest and toasted pine nuts for brightness and crunch. ([recipe](https://www.cooksillustrated.com/recipes/7375-spinach-tagliatelle-with-brown-butter-and-parmesan))

2. **Sage Brown Butter Spinach Tagliatelle** - Crisp sage leaves in the butter so the sauce turns woodsy and aromatic. ([recipe](https://www.olivemagazine.com/recipes/vegan/spinach-tagliatelle-with-sage-and-brown-butter/))

3. **Roasted Garlic Brown Butter Tagliatelle** - Mash roasted garlic into the brown butter for a mellow, savory sauce that still lets the spinach pasta show. ([recipe](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1016250-spinach-tagliatelle-with-brown-butter-and-roasted-garlic))

## Saturday - Pizza Night: Four Assorted Pies

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Smitten Kitchen](https://smittenkitchen.com/2010/05/shaved-asparagus-pizza/) |
| **Servings** | 6 to 8 |
| **Prep** | 45 minutes with prepared dough |
| **Cook** | 40 to 60 minutes total for four pies |

### Nonna Says

> Heat the oven as hot as it goes and stage every topping before the first dough stretches. Make four pies with purpose: shaved asparagus white, classic cheese, pepperoni, and mushroom garlic. Keep mushrooms away from roasted red peppers if peppers show up, because we are not starting a kitchen argument tonight. Bake each pie until the crust blisters and the cheese bubbles. Shower the asparagus pie with scallions when it comes out. Slice everything fast, feed Patrick first, and enjoy the little chaos.

### How It Comes Together

*Preheat oven to 500°F or oven maximum, pizza stone or steel inside*

**Group A → stretch and stage four crusts one at a time**

- 4 12-inch pizza dough balls *(room temperature)*

- 1/2 cup bench flour or cornmeal *(for peel)*

- 2 tablespoons olive oil *(for brushing crusts)*


**Group B → assemble shaved asparagus white pie**

- 1/2 pound asparagus *(shaved into ribbons)*

- 1/4 cup Parmesan *(grated)*

- 1/2 pound mozzarella *(shredded or cubed)*

- 2 teaspoons olive oil *(for asparagus)*

- 1 scallion *(thinly sliced)*


**Group C → assemble classic cheese and pepperoni pies**

- 1 1/2 cups tomato pizza sauce *(divided between cheese and pepperoni pies)*

- 1 pound mozzarella *(shredded)*

- 3 ounces pepperoni *(thinly sliced)*

- 1/4 cup Parmesan *(grated)*


**Group 4 → assemble mushroom garlic white pie without roasted red peppers**

- 8 ounces cremini mushrooms *(sliced and sautéed dry)*

- 2 cloves garlic *(minced)*

- 1/2 cup ricotta *(dolloped)*

- 1/2 pound mozzarella *(shredded)*


**Final assembly:** bake each pie 10 to 15 minutes until browned and bubbly → rotate halfway if needed → finish asparagus pie with scallion → rest pizzas 2 minutes → slice into wedges and serve as a four-pie spread

### Variations

1. **Shaved Asparagus and Boursin Pizza** - Swap part of the mozzarella for garlicky Boursin to make the asparagus pie creamier and tangier. ([recipe](https://www.thatsnerdalicious.com/shaved-asparagus-pizza-recipe/))

2. **Caramelized Onion, Goat Cheese, and Walnut Pizza** - Make one vegetarian pie with sweet onions, tangy goat cheese, and toasted walnuts instead of the usual red sauce. ([recipe](https://www.thekitchn.com/recipe-caramelized-onion-goat-cheese-pizza-recipes-from-the-kitchn-184322))

3. **Salami Pizza with Hot Honey** - Top a tomato and mozzarella pie with spicy salami and finish it with hot honey for sweet heat. ([recipe](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/salami-pizza-with-hot-honey-and-fresh-mozzarella))
