#### Spring Nicoise Platter with Asparagus, New Potatoes & Jammy Eggs *(Composed)*

**Cuisine:** French
A dinner-sized composed platter of spring greens, asparagus, new potatoes, radishes, jammy eggs, olives, capers, and optional tuna arranged in tidy sections rather than tossed.

**Key Ingredients:** asparagus, new potatoes, eggs, spring greens, radishes, Nicoise olives, capers

**Dressing:** Dijon-shallot vinaigrette: whisk 1/4 cup red wine vinegar, 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard, 1 small minced shallot, 1 teaspoon honey, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper; stream in 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil until emulsified, then stir in 1 tablespoon chopped capers.


**Why these flavors work:** This works because the rich eggs and potatoes need sharp acid, while asparagus and radishes bring snap and green bitterness. Olives and capers supply briny punctuation so the salad feels like a real dinner instead of a plate of vegetables.


🌿 *Asparagus, radishes, spring greens, and new potatoes are all strong East Coast late-April/early-May choices; local eggs make it more substantial.*

**Source:** [Farm Flavor](https://farmflavor.com/recipes/spring-nicoise-salad/)
*Composed dinner salad: beautiful enough for company, substantial enough for a no-cook-ish dinner, and aligned with the week's spring produce.*


#### Spring Chickpea Fattoush with Snap Peas & Crispy Pita *(Tossed)*

**Cuisine:** Middle Eastern
A generous tossed fattoush bulked up with chickpeas, romaine, cucumbers, radishes, snap peas, herbs, and shards of toasted pita.

**Key Ingredients:** chickpeas, romaine, radishes, snap peas, cucumber, mint, parsley, pita

**Dressing:** Lemon-sumac pomegranate dressing: whisk 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice, 1 tablespoon pomegranate molasses, 1 grated garlic clove, 1 teaspoon ground sumac, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper; stream in 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil and toss immediately with the salad.


**Why these flavors work:** Fattoush is all about contrast: juicy vegetables, crisp pita, creamy chickpeas, and a tart dressing that wakes everything up. Sumac and pomegranate molasses echo the Middle Eastern dinner option while the chickpeas make the bowl filling enough to stand alone.


🌿 *Radishes, tender lettuces, herbs, and snap peas are ideal late-spring Mid-Atlantic produce; cucumbers are acceptable as a crisp bridge into warmer weather.*

**Source:** [The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/fattoush-salad/)
*Tossed dinner salad: vegetarian-friendly, high-crunch, and a natural partner for falafel, branzino, or burgers.*

#### Asparagus, Radish & Sweet Pea Side Salad with Avocado-Herb Dressing
**Cuisine:** American spring
A crisp green side salad of shaved asparagus, sliced radishes, sweet peas, herbs, and feta over tender lettuces.

**Key Ingredients:** asparagus, radishes, sweet peas, butter lettuce, feta, dill, parsley

**Dressing:** Avocado-herb dressing: blend 1 ripe avocado, 3 tablespoons lemon juice, 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, 1 small garlic clove, 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tablespoons water, 1/4 cup parsley, 2 tablespoons dill, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and black pepper to taste.


**Why these flavors work:** The creamy avocado dressing softens asparagus' grassy edge and gives lean spring vegetables enough body to sit beside burgers, pork chops, or fish. Lemon and herbs keep it bright so it does not fight creamy mushroom sauce or rich cheese.


🌿 *Asparagus, radishes, peas, dill, parsley, and tender lettuces are exactly the produce to chase on the East Coast as April turns into May.*

**Source:** [Love & Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/asparagus-salad/)
*Side salad #1: a spring-green counterweight for the heavier requested dinners.*


#### Butter Lettuce, Strawberries & Pickled Shallot Salad
**Cuisine:** American spring
Soft butter lettuce, early strawberries, cucumber, toasted almonds, goat cheese, mint, and quick-pickled shallots in a poppy seed vinaigrette.

**Key Ingredients:** butter lettuce, strawberries, cucumber, shallots, almonds, goat cheese, mint

**Dressing:** Lemon-poppy seed vinaigrette: shake 3 tablespoons champagne vinegar, 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice, 1 tablespoon honey, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil, 1 teaspoon poppy seeds, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper until glossy.


**Why these flavors work:** Sweet strawberries and honey need the bite of pickled shallot and lemony acid to avoid tasting like dessert. Almonds add crunch, goat cheese adds tangy fat, and mint makes the whole salad feel cool next to pizza or spicy jambalaya.


🌿 *Butter lettuce and herbs are abundant in late April; the first Mid-Atlantic strawberries often appear around early May, and supermarket berries can stand in if local fruit lags.*

**Source:** [Love & Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/spring-salad/)
*Side salad #2: lighter, fruitier, and completely different from the asparagus-pea salad.*
