#### Spring Composed Salad with Asparagus, Peas, and Goat Cheese *(Composed)*

**Cuisine:** French
A dinner-sized composed salad arranged on a platter: blanched asparagus and sugar snap peas, fresh English peas, shaved radishes, soft goat cheese, toasted pistachios, and fresh herbs over a bed of butter lettuce and arugula.

**Key Ingredients:** asparagus, English peas, sugar snap peas, goat cheese, pistachios, butter lettuce, arugula, radishes, fresh dill, fresh mint

**Dressing:** Lemon-Herb Vinaigrette: Whisk 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil, 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp honey, 1 minced garlic clove, 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill and mint, salt and pepper to taste.


**Why these flavors work:** The bright lemon vinaigrette lifts the earthy asparagus and sweet peas, while creamy goat cheese and crunchy pistachios add richness and texture contrast. Fresh herbs tie every component to the spring garden.


🌿 *Peak East Coast April: asparagus, English peas, sugar snaps, and radishes are all at their sweetest and crunchiest right now.*

**Source:** [Cooked & Loved](https://www.cookedandloved.com/recipes/spring-mix-salad/)
*This composed beauty showcases everything April has to offer — substantial enough for a standalone dinner with crusty bread.*


#### Green Goddess Salad *(Tossed)*

**Cuisine:** American
A big, vibrant tossed dinner salad with chopped romaine, cucumber, avocado, snap peas, radishes, green onions, and fresh herbs, all coated in a creamy herb-packed Green Goddess dressing. The craving, satisfied.

**Key Ingredients:** romaine, cucumber, avocado, snap peas, radishes, green onions, chives, basil, parsley, tarragon, Greek yogurt, lemon

**Dressing:** Green Goddess Dressing: Blend 1/2 cup Greek yogurt, 1/2 cup packed fresh herbs (parsley, basil, chives, tarragon), 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 small garlic clove, 1 anchovy fillet (optional), salt and pepper until smooth and vibrant green.


**Why these flavors work:** The creamy, tangy dressing is a celebration of fresh spring herbs — tarragon and basil bring anise sweetness, chives add onion bite, and lemon keeps everything bright. Crunchy vegetables provide contrast to the lush dressing.


🌿 *Spring herbs are at their most fragrant in mid-April — tarragon, chives, and basil from the garden or farmers market make this dressing sing.*

**Source:** [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/green-goddess-salad)
*The week's craving fulfilled — paired with Saturday's burgers, this is the dinner salad that makes the whole meal feel like an event.*
