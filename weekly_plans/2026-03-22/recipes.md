## 🥗 Salads

### S01 · Green Goddess Salad with Avocado, Crispy Chickpeas & Seeds
**Type:** 🥗 Tossed Dinner Salad · **Cuisine:** American
**Source:** [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/green-goddess-salad)

A generous tossed salad of crisp romaine, shaved cucumber, green onions, and ripe avocado, coated in a vibrant herb-packed Green Goddess dressing. Topped with spiced crispy roasted chickpeas and toasted pumpkin seeds for protein and crunch.

**Key Ingredients:** romaine lettuce · cucumber · green onions · avocado · chickpeas · pumpkin seeds · chives

**Dressing:** Green Goddess dressing — fresh basil, fresh parsley, fresh chives, tarragon, 1 small garlic clove, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, ¼ cup Greek yogurt, ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil, 1 tbsp white miso paste, salt, black pepper. Blend until smooth and vivid green.

**Flavor Rationale:** The creamy herb dressing coats every leaf with bright, almost grassy freshness, while the miso adds a subtle umami backbone that makes you keep eating. Crispy chickpeas bring satisfying crunch and protein, turning a side salad into a full meal. The avocado adds richness that rounds out the acidity from lemon and vinegar.

**Seasonal Note:** Late March sees the first spring herbs arriving at East Coast markets — chives, parsley, and tarragon are at their most vibrant. Avocados are in peak season year-round. Chickpeas bridge the gap before summer proteins arrive.

### S02 · Fattoush with Za'atar Croutons & Sumac Vinaigrette
**Type:** 🍽️ Composed Dinner Salad · **Cuisine:** Middle Eastern (Lebanese)
**Source:** [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/fattoush-recipe)

An artfully arranged composed plate: torn pita crisped with za'atar and olive oil, arranged over a bed of little gem lettuce, sliced radishes, cherry tomatoes, Persian cucumbers, and fresh herbs. Drizzled with a bright sumac-lemon vinaigrette.

**Key Ingredients:** little gem lettuce · pita bread · za'atar · radishes · cherry tomatoes · Persian cucumbers · mint · parsley · sumac

**Dressing:** Sumac vinaigrette — 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice, 1 tsp ground sumac, 1 small minced garlic clove, 1 tsp pomegranate molasses, pinch of Aleppo pepper, salt to taste. Whisk until emulsified.

**Flavor Rationale:** Sumac's tart, almost berry-like acidity is the backbone — it brightens every vegetable it touches. The pomegranate molasses adds a dark, sweet-sour depth that makes the dressing complex and addictive. Za'atar croutons bring herbal crunch that echoes the fresh herbs throughout. This is the principle of layering similar flavors in different textures.

**Seasonal Note:** Radishes are among the first spring vegetables at East Coast markets. Little gem lettuce is hardy enough to be available in late March. This salad bridges winter's heartiness with spring's brightness.

### S03 · Shaved Carrot & Radish Salad with Sesame-Ginger Dressing
**Type:** 🥬 Side Salad · **Cuisine:** Japanese-inspired
**Source:** [Food52](https://food52.com/recipes/shaved-carrot-radish-salad)

Ribbons of carrot and thinly sliced watermelon radish, tossed with a nutty sesame-ginger dressing and sprinkled with toasted sesame seeds and sliced scallions. Light, crunchy, and clean — the perfect counterpoint to rich Asian mains.

**Key Ingredients:** carrots · watermelon radish · scallions · toasted sesame seeds · cilantro

### S04 · Spring Arugula Salad with Strawberries, Pecans & Goat Cheese
**Type:** 🥬 Side Salad · **Cuisine:** French
**Source:** [Epicurious](https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/arugula-strawberry-salad)

Peppery arugula tossed with sliced early-season strawberries, candied pecans, and crumbles of tangy chèvre. A classic French-inspired combination where every element has a clear role: bitter, sweet, crunchy, creamy.

**Key Ingredients:** arugula · strawberries · pecans · goat cheese · red onion

**Dressing:** Strawberry-balsamic vinaigrette — 2 ripe strawberries (mashed), 2 tbsp aged balsamic vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, 1 tsp honey, pinch of salt and cracked black pepper. Whisk until slightly thickened.

**Flavor Rationale:** The peppery bite of arugula is softened by sweet strawberries and balanced by the tangy acidity of balsamic and goat cheese. Candied pecans provide a butter-sweet crunch that contrasts the soft greens. The strawberry in the vinaigrette creates an echo effect — the same ingredient appearing in two forms — which gives the salad a sense of intentional coherence.

**Seasonal Note:** Florida strawberries are at peak season in March. Arugula is one of the first spring greens. Early pecans from fall harvest store well through spring.
