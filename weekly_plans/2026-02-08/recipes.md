### Dinner Salads

#### Winter Citrus & Radicchio Salad *(Composed)*

**Cuisine:** Italian
A stunning composed plate of blood orange and cara cara segments, shaved radicchio, creamy burrata, toasted pistachios, and Castelvetrano olives, arranged artfully on a platter.

**Key Ingredients:** blood oranges, cara cara oranges, radicchio, burrata, pistachios, Castelvetrano olives

**Dressing:** Blood orange vinaigrette: fresh blood orange juice, champagne vinegar, Dijon mustard, extra-virgin olive oil, minced shallot, flaky salt, cracked black pepper


**Why these flavors work:** Bitter radicchio is tamed by sweet citrus and creamy burrata, while the vinaigrette's acid brightens every bite. Pistachios add crunch and richness. This follows the Italian principle of balancing amaro (bitter) with dolce (sweet) and grasso (fat).


🌿 *Blood oranges and radicchio are at their peak on the East Coast in January–March.*

**Source:** [Food52](https://food52.com/recipes/77619-winter-citrus-salad)
*Stunning dinner-sized composed salad with peak-season winter citrus.*


#### Kale Caesar with Crispy Chickpeas *(Tossed)*

**Cuisine:** American
Hearty lacinato kale massaged until tender, tossed in a punchy anchovy-lemon Caesar dressing with shaved Parmesan, crunchy croutons, and spiced roasted chickpeas for protein.

**Key Ingredients:** lacinato kale, anchovy, lemon, Parmesan, chickpeas, garlic, croutons

**Dressing:** Anchovy Caesar: 2 anchovy fillets mashed, 1 clove garlic grated, juice of 1 lemon, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 egg yolk, 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil, 1/4 cup grated Parmesan, salt, pepper


**Why these flavors work:** Kale's sturdy texture stands up to the bold, umami-rich Caesar. Chickpeas bring plant protein and crunch, making this a true dinner salad. The lemon acid cuts through the rich dressing and keeps the palate fresh.


🌿 *Lacinato kale is hardy through winter and widely available on the East Coast through March.*

**Source:** [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/kale-caesar-salad)
*Hearty tossed dinner salad with winter kale and protein from chickpeas.*

#### Shaved Brussels Sprout Slaw with Apple & Pecans
**Cuisine:** American
Thinly shaved Brussels sprouts tossed with crisp Honeycrisp apple matchsticks, toasted pecans, dried cranberries, and shaved Pecorino Romano.

**Key Ingredients:** Brussels sprouts, Honeycrisp apple, pecans, dried cranberries, Pecorino Romano

**Dressing:** Apple cider vinaigrette: 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar, 1 tsp whole-grain mustard, 1 tsp honey, 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil, pinch of salt and pepper

🌿 *Brussels sprouts and stored Honeycrisp apples are at peak quality on the East Coast through February.*

**Source:** [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/shaved-brussels-sprout-salad)
*Crunchy winter side salad that pairs well with rich beef or pork mains.*


#### Roasted Beet & Citrus Salad with Goat Cheese
**Cuisine:** French
Earthy roasted beets sliced thin and fanned alongside grapefruit and navel orange segments, dolloped with tangy goat cheese and scattered with toasted walnuts.

**Key Ingredients:** red and golden beets, grapefruit, navel orange, goat cheese, walnuts, arugula

**Dressing:** Sherry-walnut vinaigrette: 2 tbsp sherry vinegar, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tbsp walnut oil, 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, minced shallot, honey, salt, pepper


**Why these flavors work:** Earthy beets and bright citrus create a classic sweet-savory tension. Goat cheese adds creamy tang, walnuts bring warmth and crunch. The sherry vinaigrette bridges the earthy and citrus notes with its own nutty sweetness.


🌿 *Beets are stored through winter and citrus peaks January–March on the East Coast.*

**Source:** [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/roasted-beet-citrus-salad)
*Valentine's-worthy color palette (red + pink + orange). French elegance as a side salad.*
