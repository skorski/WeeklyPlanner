#### Winter Citrus & Radicchio Salad *(Composed)*

**Cuisine:** Italian
Composed arrangement of blood orange and cara cara segments, shaved radicchio, creamy burrata, toasted pistachios, and Castelvetrano olives on a platter. A stunning centerpiece salad that's hearty enough for dinner with crusty bread.

**Key Ingredients:** blood oranges, cara cara oranges, radicchio, burrata, pistachios, Castelvetrano olives

**Dressing:** Blood orange vinaigrette: fresh blood orange juice, champagne vinegar, Dijon mustard, extra-virgin olive oil, minced shallot, flaky salt, cracked black pepper, pinch of honey


**Why these flavors work:** The bitter radicchio is tamed by sweet citrus segments and creamy burrata, while the acid in the vinaigrette brightens every bite. Pistachios add crunch and richness. This follows the Italian principle of balancing amaro (bitter) with dolce (sweet) and grasso (fat).


🌿 *Blood oranges and radicchio are at their peak on the East Coast in January–March. Cara cara oranges add a pink, seedless sweetness.*


*Stunning composed dinner salad with peak-season winter citrus. Italian theme alignment.*

### Side Salads


#### Shaved Brussels Sprout Slaw with Pecorino & Hazelnuts
**Cuisine:** Italian-American
Thinly shaved Brussels sprouts tossed with shaved Pecorino Romano, toasted hazelnuts, and a bright lemon-anchovy dressing. Crunchy, savory, and the perfect counterpoint to rich pasta or roasted chicken.

**Key Ingredients:** Brussels sprouts, Pecorino Romano, hazelnuts, lemon, anchovy

**Dressing:** Lemon-anchovy dressing: fresh lemon juice, minced anchovy (or anchovy paste), Dijon mustard, extra-virgin olive oil, finely grated garlic, salt, black pepper


**Why these flavors work:** The mild bitterness and crunch of raw Brussels sprouts pairs beautifully with the umami punch of anchovy and the sharp saltiness of Pecorino. Lemon brightens everything, while hazelnuts add a toasty warmth. This is umami + acid + crunch at work.


🌿 *Brussels sprouts are a prime winter vegetable on the East Coast, sweetest after the first frosts of the season.*


*Perfect side salad for pasta or chicken nights. Brussels sprouts are at their winter peak.*


#### Simple Arugula & Parmesan Salad with Lemon Vinaigrette
**Cuisine:** Italian
Peppery arugula tossed with shaved Parmigiano-Reggiano, a squeeze of fresh lemon, and the best olive oil you have. The ultimate 3-minute side salad that elevates any dinner to restaurant-level.

**Key Ingredients:** arugula, Parmigiano-Reggiano, lemon, extra-virgin olive oil

**Dressing:** Simple lemon vinaigrette: fresh lemon juice, extra-virgin olive oil, flaky Maldon salt, cracked black pepper — whisked together just before serving


**Why these flavors work:** Arugula's natural pepperiness is softened by the rich nuttiness of Parmigiano-Reggiano and the fruity acidity of lemon and good olive oil. This salad works because every ingredient is pulling its weight — there's nothing to hide behind.


🌿 *Arugula is available year-round via hydroponic farming. Lemons are at their best in winter months.*


*The go-to side salad — pairs with literally any dinner on this list. Fast, simple, perfect.*
