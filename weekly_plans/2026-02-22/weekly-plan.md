#### Dinner: Sunday Gravy and Pasta

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Italian-American |

Italian-American Sunday gravy with mixed meats slow-simmered in tomato sauce, served over rigatoni with garlic bread.

*Dan on call as Incident Manager. Snow day family cooking.*





##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** First thing, you brown your meats. Every single piece gets a hard sear in that pot. Don't crowd it or nothing gets color. Take them out, same pot, get those onions in there. Low and slow till they're sweet. Garlic goes in, and if you can't smell it through the whole house, you didn't use enough. Tomato paste, stir it around till it gets dark. Now the San Marzanos, crush them with your hands right into the pot. Everything goes back in, bring it up to a bubble, then low. Real low. Three, four hours. You stir when you think about it. The house will tell you when it's done.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — brown all sides in batches, remove**
- 1 lb Italian sausage *(whole links)*
- 1 lb pork ribs *(separated)*
- 3 tbsp olive oil

**Step 2 — soften onion 5 min, garlic 1 min, toast paste 2 min**
- 1 large onion *(diced)*
- 4 cloves garlic *(sliced)*
- 3 tbsp tomato paste

**Step 3 — add to pot, stir**
- 2 (28-oz) cans San Marzano tomatoes *(hand-crushed)*
- 1 cup water *(to rinse cans)*
- 4-5 leaves fresh basil

**Final:** return all meats → bring to simmer → cover, low heat 3-4 hours → stir occasionally → season and serve over rigatoni

**Variations:**
- **Bolognese Ragu** — Skip the big meat pieces, use a mix of ground beef, pork, and veal with milk and white wine for a Emilia-Romagna style meat sauce ([recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-best-slow-cooked-bolognese-sauce-recipe))
- **Braciole Sunday Gravy** — Add thin beef rolls stuffed with breadcrumbs, garlic, parsley, and pecorino — they simmer in the sauce like little presents ([recipe](https://www.gonnawantseconds.com/italian-sunday-gravy-recipe/))
- **Vegetarian Sunday Sauce** — Replace meats with portobello mushrooms, eggplant, and white beans — long simmer still builds the depth ([recipe](https://oursaltykitchen.com/sunday-sauce-with-homemade-meatballs/))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** Brown the Meats in Stages — Sear meats individually in a screaming-hot Dutch oven with canola oil. Get a hard, dark crust on each piece before removing. This fond is liquid gold. 3-4 minutes per side, undisturbed.
- **SAUCE:** Parmigiano Rind in the Gravy — Drop a 3-inch piece of Parmigiano-Reggiano rind into the gravy during the last 90 minutes. It softens, releases umami, and adds silky body.
- **ACID:** Fresh Basil and Good Olive Oil Finish — Tear 8-10 fresh basil leaves over plated pasta. Finish with a thread of your best extra-virgin olive oil.
- **TEXTURE:** Garlic Butter Toast — Mix softened butter with grated garlic, flaky salt, and minced parsley. Spread on thick bread slices and broil until golden (90 seconds max).

#### Dinner: Shepherds Pie

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | British |
| **Key Ingredients** | ground beef, peas, carrots, potatoes, tomato paste, red wine, Worcestershire |
| **Source** | [Yellow Bliss Road](https://www.yellowblissroad.com/easy-shepherds-pie-recipe/) |
Classic comfort: ground beef with peas and carrots under golden fork-raked mashed potato crust.

*School likely cancelled (snow). Dan on call AM.*





##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** Boil your potatoes till they're soft, and salt that water like you mean it. Mash them with butter and milk till they're like clouds. Brown the beef good, break it up, don't leave big chunks. Onion, carrots, peas, let everything get friendly in there. Tomato paste, a good splash of Worcestershire, some broth, let it thicken up. You want it saucy, not soupy. Everything goes in the dish, potatoes on top. Now make little peaks with your fork. That's where the gold happens, those peaks get all brown and crispy. Oven, 400, wait till it's bubbling at the edges. Don't you dare take it out early.


**How It Comes Together**
*Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C)*

**Step 1 — boil 15 min, drain, mash smooth**
- 2.5 lbs potatoes *(peeled, cubed)*
- 4 tbsp butter
- ½ cup milk *(warm)*
- 1 tsp salt

**Step 2 — brown beef, drain fat, sauté veg 5 min**
- 1.5 lbs ground beef
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion *(diced)*
- 2 carrots *(diced)*

**Step 3 — stir in, simmer 8-10 min until thick**
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 cup frozen peas

**Final:** spread filling in baking dish → top with mashed potatoes → fork peaks for browning → bake 25-30 min until golden

**Variations:**
- **Lamb Shepherd's Pie** — Use ground lamb with fresh rosemary and a splash of red wine for the authentic British version ([recipe](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/shepherds_pie/))
- **Sweet Potato Topped** — Swap regular mashed potatoes for roasted sweet potato mash with a hint of cinnamon ([recipe](https://www.wellplated.com/sweet-potato-shepherds-pie/))
- **Guinness Cottage Pie** — Deglaze the meat with Guinness stout and add extra root veg for an Irish pub classic ([recipe](https://www.thespruceeats.com/guinness-cottage-pie-recipe-435689))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** Bloom Your Tomato Paste — After browning meat, push aside and add 2 tbsp tomato paste to bare pan. Cook 2 full minutes until brick-red and sweet-smelling, then stir into meat.
- **SAUCE:** Red Wine and Worcestershire — Pour half cup dry red wine and reduce by half. Add generous tablespoon of Worcestershire with the stock.
- **TEXTURE:** Fork-Raked Top with Butter Dots — Drag fork across potato surface in crosshatch. Dot with small pieces of cold butter. Bake at 400F until ridges are deeply golden.
- **ACID:** Fresh Thyme and Flaky Salt — Scatter fresh thyme leaves and Maldon salt over golden top right out of the oven.

#### Dinner: Babo Pasta with Alfredo Sauce

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Italian |
| **Key Ingredients** | babo pasta (spinach egg-yolk), heavy cream, butter, parmesan, garlic, nutmeg |
| **Source** | [The Clever Carrot](https://www.theclevercarrot.com/2022/05/fresh-spinach-pasta-dough/) |
Homemade spinach egg-yolk pasta tossed in a creamy Alfredo sauce with parmesan and a touch of nutmeg.

*Dan off-call Tuesday afternoon. Swim lessons evening.*





##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** This one is simple and you are not going to mess it up. Blanch your spinach, thirty seconds, ice water, squeeze every drop out. Chop it fine. Make a mountain of flour on your board, push a well in the middle. Eggs, yolks, spinach, pour it in. Fork first, then your hands. Knead it till it is smooth as silk, ten minutes at least. For the sauce, melt butter with a clove of garlic, low heat. Pull the garlic when it is golden. Pour in the cream, let it barely simmer. Toss the pasta in with a good handful of parmesan and a scrape of nutmeg. Add pasta water if it needs loosening. Eat it while it is hot.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — blanch 30 sec, ice bath, squeeze bone-dry, chop fine**
- 3 cups (90g) fresh baby spinach *(packed)*
- 1 pot boiling water

**Step 2 — well method, incorporate spinach-egg mix, knead 10 min**
- 3 cups (425g) 00 flour *(mounded)*
- 5 large egg yolks
- 2 large whole eggs
- pinch kosher salt

**Step 3 — melt butter with garlic (remove garlic), add cream, simmer 2 min, stir in parmesan off heat**
- 3 Tbs butter
- 1 clove garlic *(smashed)*
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup parmesan *(finely grated)*
- pinch nutmeg *(freshly grated)*

**Final:** toss pasta in Alfredo sauce → add pasta water to loosen → plate and finish with parmesan

**Variations:**
- **Fettuccine Alfredo Classico** — Use fettuccine instead of spinach pasta for the Roman original ([recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/fettuccine-alfredo-recipe))
- **Alfredo with Roasted Garlic** — Swap raw garlic for a full roasted head mashed into the cream sauce ([recipe](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/roasted-garlic-alfredo))
- **Lemon Cream Pasta** — Add lemon zest and juice to the Alfredo for brightness, finish with fresh basil ([recipe](https://www.loveandlemons.com/lemon-pasta/))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** Rest Dough 45 Minutes — Rest wrapped dough for full 45 minutes at room temp. The extra time lets gluten relax completely for thinner, more even rolling.
- **SAUCE:** Silky Alfredo Finish — Reserve 1 cup pasta water. Toss hot pasta with butter, cream, and finely grated parmesan off-heat, adding pasta water a splash at a time until glossy and clinging. A pinch of nutmeg ties it together.
- **TEXTURE:** Toasted Pine Nuts and Microplaned Parm — Toast pine nuts in dry pan until golden (3-4 min, watch carefully). Scatter over pasta. Grate Parmigiano on Microplane so it falls like snow.
- **ACID:** Lemon Zest Shower — Zest half a lemon directly over each plate using Microplane just before serving.

#### Dinner: Niku Udon

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Japanese |
| **Key Ingredients** | udon noodles, kombu, katsuobushi, ribeye, soy sauce, mirin, eggs, scallions, nori |

Chewy udon in real dashi with soy-mirin glazed beef, onsen egg, and toppings bar.

*Elsie science potions camp 3:45-4:45 PM.*





##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** This one isn't mine, it's from another grandmother far away, but she'd tell you the same thing I would: start with good broth. Kombu goes in cold water, low heat. Pull it out before it boils or it gets slimy and bitter. Bonito flakes go in, steep two minutes, strain. That's your dashi. For the beef, slice it paper thin. Cook it fast with soy, mirin, and a little sugar. Don't overdo it or it turns into shoe leather. Noodles, hot broth, beef on top, scallions. Simple things done right.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — kombu in cold water, heat to just below boil, remove kombu, add bonito, steep 2 min, strain**
- 4 cups water
- 4-inch piece kombu *(wiped)*
- 20g katsuobushi (bonito)

**Step 2 — sauté onion, add beef quickly, season, cook 2-3 min**
- 8 oz ribeye *(sliced paper-thin)*
- ½ onion *(thin sliced)*
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp mirin
- ½ tbsp sugar

**Step 3 — cook noodles per package**
- 200g udon noodles *(fresh or frozen)*
- 2 scallions *(sliced)*

**Final:** season dashi with 2 tbsp mirin + 1-2 tbsp soy + ½ tsp salt → noodles in bowls → ladle hot broth over → top with beef and scallions

**Variations:**
- **Curry Udon (Kare Udon)** — Thicken the dashi broth with Japanese curry roux for a warming, spiced version ([recipe](https://www.justonecookbook.com/curry-udon/))
- **Sukiyaki Style** — Cook the beef and onions sukiyaki-style with sake, soy, and sugar, then serve over udon in lighter broth ([recipe](https://www.justonecookbook.com/sukiyaki/))
- **Kimchi Udon** — Add fermented kimchi and gochugaru to the broth for a Korean-Japanese fusion bowl with funky heat ([recipe](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/kimchi-udon-with-scallions))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** Build Real Dashi — Bring 4 cups water with 4-inch kombu piece to just below boil (never boil kombu). Remove, add packed cup of katsuobushi, steep 5 min, strain. Season with soy, mirin, pinch of sugar.
- **SAUCE:** Soy-Mirin Glazed Beef — Slice beef paper-thin against grain. Sear in batches 30 sec per side. Deglaze with 2 tbsp each soy and mirin. Reduce to glaze (30 sec), toss beef to coat.
- **TEMPERATURE:** Onsen Egg — Hold water at 167F for 13 minutes with eggs. Transfer to ice water. Crack over finished udon - white barely set, yolk completely liquid.
- **TEXTURE:** Toppings Bar — Set out shichimi togarashi, torn nori, sliced scallions, and tenkasu (crispy tempura bits) for everyone to customize.

Milk goes in slow, keep whisking. When it's thick like cream, off the heat. Three cheeses: cheddar for flavor, Gruyère because we have standards, and American because it melts like a dream. #### Dinner: Linguini with Clams

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Italian |
| **Key Ingredients** | linguini, littleneck clams, garlic, white wine, olive oil, breadcrumbs, lemon, parsley |
| **Source** | [Sip and Feast](https://www.sipandfeast.com/linguine-alle-vongole/) |
Classic linguini alle vongole: clams in white wine with garlic three ways and pangritata.

*Elsie YAT 4:30-6:00 PM.*





##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** First, and I cannot stress this enough, soak your clams in salty water. Thirty minutes minimum. Any sand in your pasta and dinner is ruined. Heat olive oil, good olive oil, and let the garlic go golden. Not brown, golden. A pinch of red pepper if you like. In go all the clams, pour the wine over, lid on tight. Five minutes, they open. Take them out right away. Let that beautiful juice reduce a little. The pasta goes in that juice with a splash of pasta water, toss it until the noodles drink everything up. Clams back in, parsley, a thread of your best olive oil. No cheese. Never cheese with clams.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — heat oil, sauté garlic 1 min, add clams + wine, cover 5-7 min**
- 2 lbs littleneck clams *(scrubbed, purged 30 min)*
- ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 3-4 cloves garlic *(thinly sliced)*
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- ¾ cup dry white wine

**Step 2 — drain, reserve 1 cup pasta water**
- 1 lb linguine *(cook 1 min short of al dente)*

**Step 3 — ready for finishing**
- ¼ cup fresh Italian parsley *(chopped)*
- drizzle finishing olive oil

**Final:** remove clams from broth, reduce broth 2 min → add pasta to broth, toss with pasta water → return clams, add parsley + oil → serve immediately, no cheese

**Variations:**
- **Linguine alle Vongole in Rosso** — Add San Marzano tomatoes to the clam sauce for the red version — equally classic in Naples ([recipe](https://www.the-pasta-project.com/linguine-alle-vongole-in-rosso/))
- **Spaghetti with Mussels** — Swap clams for mussels and add a splash of cream and fresh tarragon for a French-Italian crossover ([recipe](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/spaghetti-with-mussels))
- **Clams Casino Pasta** — Top with crispy breadcrumbs, bacon bits, and roasted peppers for an Italian-American bar-style twist ([recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/clams-casino-recipe))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** Finish Pasta in Clam Broth — Steam clams open first, reserve and strain all liquor. Cook linguini 2 min short, finish directly in clam broth with pasta water. Starch-clam liquor creates silky, briny sauce.
- **SAUCE:** Garlic Three Ways — (1) Thinly sliced garlic cooked golden in olive oil as base. (2) One clove grated raw into sauce at end. (3) Garlic-infused breadcrumbs as finish. Three textures, three intensities.
- **TEXTURE:** Pangritata (Crispy Garlic Breadcrumbs) — Toast coarse breadcrumbs in olive oil with minced garlic, red pepper flakes, and lemon zest until deeply golden. Scatter over plated pasta.
- **ACID:** White Wine Reduction and Lemon Finish — Use dry Vermentino or Pinot Grigio, reduce by two-thirds before adding clam broth. Finish with lemon squeeze and rough-chopped parsley.

##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** Three days before, you make this dough. Flour, water, a whisper of yeast, salt. Mix it, knead it a little, into the fridge. Three days. The yeast does slow, beautiful work. On pizza night, pull it out three hours early. Divide it into three balls. Stretch with your hands, never a rolling pin. For the buffalo chicken: shredded chicken, hot sauce, blue cheese, mozzarella. For the classic: good sauce, mozzarella, pepperoni on top so the edges curl. The mac and cheese one is ridiculous but the kids will love you forever. Hot oven, as hot as it goes. Six, seven minutes. Watch it.


**How It Comes Together**
*Preheat oven to Oven max (500-550°F) with pizza stone/steel*

**Step 1 — mix flour+yeast, add water, rest 30 min, add salt, knead**
- 500g bread flour
- 350g water *(room temp)*
- 16g sea salt
- 1g (¼ tsp) instant yeast

**Step 2 — toss chicken in sauce (Pie 1 topping)**
- 1 cup buffalo sauce
- 2 cups shredded chicken *(cooked)*
- ½ cup gorgonzola *(crumbled)*

**Step 3 — classic assembly (Pie 2 topping)**
- 1 cup pizza sauce *(San Marzano)*

**Step 4 — spread on dough (Pie 3 topping)**

**Final:** bulk rise 1-2 hrs, cold ferment 72 hrs → divide into 3 balls, warm 3 hrs → stretch by hand, add toppings → bake on stone/steel 6-8 min each

**Variations:**
- **Detroit-Style Pan Pizza** — Press dough into an oiled steel pan, cheese to the edges for caramelized corners, sauce on top ([recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/detroit-style-pizza-recipe))
- **Grilled Pizza** — Stretch dough, grill one side directly on grates, flip, add toppings, cover to melt ([recipe](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/grilled-pizza))
- **Pizza Bianca with Potatoes** — Skip tomato sauce entirely — olive oil, thinly sliced potatoes, rosemary, sea salt on the crust ([recipe](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/pizza-bianca-recipe))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** 72-Hour Cold Fermented Dough — Make dough 3 days ahead. Bread flour, 65% hydration, 1/4 tsp yeast per 500g flour, 3% salt. Refrigerate 72 hours. Pull out 2 hours before baking.
- **SAUCE:** Blue Cheese Bechamel for Buffalo Pie — Make light bechamel with butter, flour, milk. Off heat, crumble in Gorgonzola dolce until melted. Spread as base. Top with buffalo chicken, mozz, capers, thin celery slices.
- **TEXTURE:** Cup-and-Char Pepperoni — Use natural-casing pepperoni sliced thick. The casing causes edges to curl into cups that pool with rendered spicy fat. Place on top of cheese.
- **TEMPERATURE:** Hot Honey Drizzle — After pulling pepperoni pie from oven, drizzle with hot honey (3 tbsp honey + 1 tsp red pepper flakes, warmed).
