## Sunday — Pulled Pork Picnic with Cole Slaw on Taco Shells

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [RecipeTeacher](https://recipeteacher.com/best-damn-slow-cooker-pulled-pork/) |
| **Servings** | 6-8 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 8 hours (slow cooker) |

### Nonna Says

> Listen here, honey. You rub that pork shoulder like you mean it. Brown sugar, smoked paprika, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper. Every inch. Put it in the slow cooker, set it to low, and walk away for eight hours. When it's done, the meat should fall apart if you even look at it. Shred it with two forks, toss it back in those juices, and pile it high on your taco shells. The slaw goes on top. Cabbage, carrots, a little vinegar, a pinch of sugar, and some mayo. If you skip the slaw, you're eating dry pork in a shell and that's not a meal, that's a punishment.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → mix rub, massage all over pork**

- 3-4 lb pork shoulder (butt) *(patted dry)*

- 2 Tbs brown sugar

- 1 Tbs smoked paprika

- 1 Tbs chili powder

- 1 tsp garlic powder

- 1 tsp salt

- 1 tsp black pepper

- 1 tsp dry mustard


**Group B → place in slow cooker bottom**

- 1/2 cup chicken broth

- 1 onion *(quartered)*


**Group C → toss together, chill until serving**

- 3 cups green cabbage *(shredded thin)*

- 1 cup carrots *(grated)*

- 1/3 cup mayonnaise

- 1 Tbs apple cider vinegar

- 1 tsp sugar

- pinch salt and pepper


**Final assembly:** place rubbed pork on onions in slow cooker → cook LOW 8 hrs until fork-tender → shred with two forks, toss in juices → pile pork on taco shells, top with slaw

### Variations

1. **Korean Gochujang Pulled Pork** — Replace the dry rub with a gochujang, soy sauce, sesame oil, and honey glaze for a sweet-spicy Korean spin ([recipe](https://www.theendlessmeal.com/crock-pot-korean-gochujang-pork/))

2. **Carolina Vinegar Pulled Pork** — Skip the dry rub sweetness and finish with a tangy vinegar, red pepper flake, and black pepper mop sauce ([recipe](https://www.foodiecrush.com/carolina-pulled-pork-vinegar-bbq-sauce/))

3. **Hawaiian Kalua Pulled Pork** — Use just sea salt and liquid smoke for a smoky island-style pork, serve on Hawaiian sweet rolls with pineapple slaw ([recipe](https://damndelicious.net/2014/07/12/slow-cooker-kalua-pig/))

## Monday — Chickpea & Broccoli Curry over Rice

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Vegan Cocotte](https://vegancocotte.com/broccoli-chickpea-curry/) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 30 min |

### Nonna Says

> Heat the coconut oil in your big pan. Medium heat. Onions go in first until they're soft and sweet. Then garlic, ginger, and that little chili pepper if you're brave. Stir in your spices, cumin, coriander, chili powder, turmeric, and let them toast for one minute, no more. Tomato paste, then the whole can of coconut milk, stir it smooth. In go the broccoli and chickpeas, push them under the sauce. Simmer ten minutes until the broccoli still has some fight in it. Finish with coconut cream and cilantro. Anyone who says broccoli is boring just hasn't met this curry.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → saute onion 3-5 min, add garlic/ginger/chili 1 min**

- 1 Tbs coconut oil

- 1 medium onion *(diced)*

- 2 cloves garlic *(minced)*

- 1 Tbs fresh ginger *(finely chopped)*

- 1/4 red chili pepper *(finely chopped)*


**Group B → stir spices 1 min, add paste and milk, simmer**

- 1 tsp ground cumin

- 1 tsp ground coriander

- 1/2 tsp chili powder

- 1/2 tsp turmeric

- 2 Tbs tomato paste

- 14 oz can coconut milk


**Group C → add broccoli and chickpeas, simmer 10 min**

- 1 lb broccoli florets *(cut small)*

- 14 oz can chickpeas *(drained and rinsed)*

- 1/2 cup coconut cream

- handful fresh cilantro *(roughly chopped)*


**Final assembly:** stir in coconut cream and cilantro → season with salt to taste → serve over steamed rice

*Meanwhile, in a second pot:*

**bring to boil, cover, simmer 15 min**

- 1.5 cups basmati rice *(rinsed)*

- 2.25 cups water

- pinch salt


**Then:** fluff with fork → serve curry over rice

### Variations

1. **Thai Coconut Chickpea Curry** — Swap the Indian spices for Thai red curry paste, add bamboo shoots and basil for a Southeast Asian take ([recipe](https://vegancocotte.com/thai-coconut-chickpea-curry/))

2. **Curried Chickpeas with Spinach** — Drop the broccoli and add spinach with extra tomato sauce for a more traditional Indian chana palak ([recipe](https://www.budgetbytes.com/curried-chickpeas-spinach/))

3. **Japanese Curry with Chickpeas and Vegetables** — Use Japanese curry roux blocks instead of individual spices for a sweeter, thicker curry with potatoes and carrots ([recipe](https://www.justonecookbook.com/vegetarian-japanese-curry/))

## Tuesday — Babo Pasta (Spinach Egg Yolk Pasta with Alfredo)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Belly Full (Alfredo base)](https://bellyfull.net/homemade-alfredo-sauce/) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 30 min (pasta) + 5 min (sauce) |
| **Cook** | 15 min |

### Nonna Says

> This is Babo's pasta, so pay attention. Spinach goes in the food processor until it's smooth. Make a well of flour on the counter. Egg yolks only, no whites, and that spinach puree go in the middle. Work it together with your hands until it's a smooth green dough, then let it rest. Roll it thin, cut it how you like it. For the sauce, melt butter, pour in the cream, let it warm up gentle. Parmesan goes in off the heat, a little at a time, stirring always. This is not hard. If it breaks, your heat was too high and I told you to keep it low.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → well method: yolks and spinach puree in flour well, knead 8-10 min, rest 30 min**

- 2 cups all-purpose flour (or 00) *(mounded on counter)*

- 4 large egg yolks

- 2 cups packed fresh spinach *(pureed smooth with 1 Tbs water)*

- pinch salt


**Group B → melt butter, add cream, simmer 5 min, remove from heat, stir in cheese**

- 1/2 cup unsalted butter

- 2 cups heavy cream

- 1.5 cups Parmigiano-Reggiano *(freshly grated)*

- pinch nutmeg *(freshly grated)*

- to taste salt and white pepper


**Final assembly:** roll pasta thin, cut into fettuccine or pappardelle → boil in salted water 2-3 min (fresh cooks fast) → toss pasta in Alfredo sauce with splash of pasta water → serve immediately with extra Parmesan

### Variations

1. **Pesto Spinach Alfredo** — Stir 2 tablespoons of basil pesto into the Alfredo sauce for a vibrant, herby green version ([recipe](https://www.thereciperebel.com/pesto-spinach-alfredo-sauce/))

2. **Lemon Cream Pasta** — Add lemon zest and juice to the Alfredo for a bright, citrusy twist that cuts through the richness ([recipe](https://savorysofia.com/spinach-alfredo-pasta/))

3. **Roasted Garlic Alfredo** — Roast a whole head of garlic until soft and sweet, mash it into the butter before building the cream sauce ([recipe](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/231860/spinach-alfredo-sauce-better-than-olive-garden/))

## Wednesday — One-Pan Chicken Broccoli Couscous

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Hungry Happens](https://hungryhappens.net/one-pan-lemon-chicken-broccoli-couscous/) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |

### Nonna Says

> Season those chicken thighs with salt, garlic powder, onion powder. Skin side down in hot olive oil, don't move them for six minutes. You want golden, not pale. Flip, four more minutes, then out. Same pan, butter and lemon slices go in, sear them quick. Shallots, broccoli, couscous, toast it all two minutes. Garlic, wine, broth, Italian herbs, everything in. Chicken goes back on top, nestled in nice. Lid on, simmer twelve minutes. Dan, this is a Wednesday night dinner that looks like you tried. And you barely had to.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → season chicken, sear skin-down 6 min, flip 4 min, set aside**

- 4 bone-in skin-on chicken thighs *(patted dry)*

- to taste salt, garlic powder, onion powder

- 2 Tbs olive oil *(divided)*


**Group B → sear lemon slices both sides, saute shallot 2 min**

- 1 Tbs butter

- 1 small lemon *(thin sliced, seeds removed)*

- 1 large shallot *(sliced)*


**Group C → add broccoli and couscous, toast 2 min, garlic 30 sec, wine 1 min, then broth and herbs**

- 4 cups broccoli *(small florets)*

- 1.25 cups pearl couscous

- 4 cloves garlic *(minced)*

- 1/2 cup dry white wine

- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth

- 1 tsp Italian herb blend


**Final assembly:** return lemon slices and chicken to pan → bring to boil, lid on, simmer 12-14 min → uncover, cook 2 more min → garnish with fresh parsley

### Variations

1. **Mediterranean Chicken Couscous with Pistachio Gremolata** — Add bell peppers, olives, and smoked paprika, finish with a pistachio-parsley-lemon gremolata ([recipe](https://dishingouthealth.com/mediterranean-chicken-and-couscous/))

2. **Greek Lemon Chicken with Pearl Couscous and Feta** — Swap broccoli for spinach, add oregano and crumbled feta, finish with lemon zest ([recipe](https://meadowspoon.com/recipes/creamy-greek-chicken-pearl-couscous))

3. **Harissa Chicken Couscous** — Marinate chicken in harissa paste and citrus, add carrots and serve with yogurt on the side for a North African spin ([recipe](https://newkitchencedargrove.com/chicken-couscous-recipes/))

## Thursday — American Tacos with Costco Taco Seasoning

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Once Upon a Chef](https://www.onceuponachef.com/recipes/beef-tacos.html) |
| **Servings** | 4-6 (about 12 tacos) |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |

### Nonna Says

> Brown the beef in a hot pan. Break it up good, no big chunks. Drain the fat if there's too much, but leave a little for flavor. Sprinkle the Costco taco seasoning packet over the meat, pour in a splash of water, and let it simmer until the sauce gets thick and coats everything. That's it. Warm your shells. Set out the toppings. Lettuce, tomato, cheese, sour cream. Let Elsie build her own. She'll eat twice as much when she gets to pick what goes in it. See? Taco Thursday is the easiest night of the week.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → brown beef and onion 5-7 min, drain excess fat, add seasoning and water, simmer 3-5 min**

- 1 Tbs olive oil

- 1 lb ground beef (80/20)

- 1/2 onion *(finely diced (optional))*

- 1 packet Kirkland (Costco) taco seasoning

- 1/2 cup water


**Group B → warm in oven at 325F for 3 min**

- 12 crunchy taco shells or flour tortillas *(warmed)*


**Group C → set out in bowls for taco bar**

- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

- 2 cups shredded lettuce

- 2 tomatoes *(diced)*

- 1/2 cup sour cream

- 1/2 cup salsa

- 1 avocado *(sliced (optional))*


**Final assembly:** spoon meat into warmed shells → let everyone top their own → serve with extra salsa and lime wedges

### Variations

1. **Korean Beef Tacos** — Season ground beef with soy sauce, sesame oil, gochujang, and brown sugar, top with kimchi slaw and sriracha mayo ([recipe](https://damndelicious.net/2018/10/12/korean-beef-tacos/))

2. **Smash Burger Tacos** — Press seasoned beef balls onto a hot skillet directly on flour tortillas for crispy edges, top with burger fixings ([recipe](https://www.tasteofhome.com/recipes/smash-burger-tacos/))

3. **Black Bean and Corn Tacos** — Skip the beef entirely, use seasoned black beans and charred corn for a quick vegetarian taco night ([recipe](https://www.loveandlemons.com/black-bean-tacos/))

## Friday — Mac & Cheese with Broccoli

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Feel Good Foodie](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/recipe/broccoli-mac-and-cheese/) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |

### Nonna Says

> Boil your pasta in good salty water. Steam the broccoli right on top in a colander. In another pot, melt the butter, whisk in the flour, let it go golden two minutes. Now the milk goes in, warm milk if you're smart, and whisk until it thickens. Dijon mustard, just a little. Then the cheese, cheddar and gruyere, low heat, stir it in handful by handful. Fold in the pasta and broccoli. Eat it now while it's hot and perfect. If you want to get fancy, put some breadcrumbs on top and run it under the broiler. But it's Friday night, honey, you've done enough this week.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → boil pasta al dente, steam broccoli on top 1-2 min, drain pasta, blanch broccoli**

- 8 oz elbow macaroni

- 3 cups broccoli florets


**Group B → melt butter, whisk in flour 2 min, add warm milk, simmer and whisk 5-7 min until thick**

- 2 Tbs salted butter

- 2 Tbs all-purpose flour

- 2 cups 2% milk *(warmed)*

- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

- to taste salt and pepper


**Group C → stir into sauce off heat until melted and smooth**

- 1 cup cheddar cheese *(shredded from block)*

- 1 cup gruyere cheese *(shredded from block)*


**Final assembly:** add Dijon mustard to sauce → fold in cooked pasta and broccoli → serve immediately

### Variations

1. **Buffalo Chicken Broccoli Mac** — Add shredded rotisserie chicken tossed in buffalo sauce, swap some cheddar for blue cheese crumbles ([recipe](https://tasteandsee.com/buffalo-chicken-mac-cheese/))

2. **Butternut Squash Mac and Cheese** — Blend roasted butternut squash into the cheese sauce for hidden veggies, extra sweetness, and gorgeous color ([recipe](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/recipe/butternut-squash-mac-cheese/))

3. **Baked Mac and Cheese with Crispy Panko Top** — Transfer to a baking dish, top with panko breadcrumbs and extra Parmesan, bake at 375F until golden and bubbly ([recipe](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/recipe/baked-macaroni-and-cheese/))

## Saturday — Pizza Night after Elsie's YAT Play

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | Family Tradition |
| **Servings** | 3 |
| **Prep** | 5 min (ordering) |
| **Cook** | 0 min (delivery) |

### Nonna Says

> Tonight we celebrate. Elsie was on that stage and she was magnificent, and nobody is cooking. You call the pizza place. Get one cheese for the little one because she earned it. Get one with something good for the grown-ups, pepperoni, sausage, whatever makes you happy. When it arrives, put it on real plates. Light a candle. Pour the drinks. Just because it came in a box doesn't mean it can't feel special. And save a slice for tomorrow because cold pizza for breakfast is a reward for a good week.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → order from favorite local spot 30 min before showtime ends**

- 1 large cheese pizza *(for Elsie)*

- 1 specialty pizza (pepperoni, sausage, or veggie) *(for Dan and Laura)*


**Group B → toss salad while pizza is en route**

- 1 bag pre-made side salad *(optional but recommended)*

- 1 bottle Italian dressing or ranch


**Group C → set a proper table because tonight matters**

- 1 candle *(for the table)*

- 3 real plates (not paper)

- 1 beverage of choice *(sparkling water, juice, or wine)*


**Final assembly:** open the boxes with ceremony → serve on real plates with salad → toast Elsie's performance → save leftovers for Saturday morning cold pizza breakfast

### Variations

1. **Homemade Flatbread Pizza** — Use store-bought naan or pita as a quick base, let everyone top their own mini pizzas and bake 10 min ([recipe](https://www.loveandlemons.com/flatbread-pizza/))

2. **Pizza Quesadillas** — Spread pizza sauce and mozzarella inside flour tortillas, cook in a skillet for a crispy pizza-quesadilla hybrid ([recipe](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/pizzadilla/))
