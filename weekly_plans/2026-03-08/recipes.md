#### Mediterranean Chickpea Salad with Lemon-Herb Dressing *(Composed)*

**Cuisine:** Mediterranean
An artfully arranged platter of tender chickpeas, halved grape tomatoes, crisp cucumber, briny Kalamata olives, and pickled red onions, showered with fresh parsley, dill, and mint. Substantial enough for a standalone dinner with crusty bread.

**Key Ingredients:** chickpeas, grape tomatoes, cucumber, Kalamata olives, pickled red onions, parsley, dill, mint

**Dressing:** Bright lemon vinaigrette: 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 small garlic clove (minced), 1/2 tsp kosher salt, freshly cracked black pepper. Whisk together at the bottom of a large bowl before adding ingredients.


**Why these flavors work:** The acid from lemon and brine from olives cut through the starchy richness of chickpeas. Fresh herbs add aromatic brightness, while pickled onions provide a tangy, sweet counterpoint. This is the Mediterranean principle of balancing earthy legumes with sharp, bright flavors — fat (olive oil) + acid (lemon) + salt (olives) + sweet (pickled onion) in every bite.


🌿 *Early March on the East Coast: greenhouse herbs, stored garlic, and pantry chickpeas are at their best. Hothouse cucumbers and early grape tomatoes are available and improving in quality as spring approaches.*

**Source:** [Love & Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/chickpea-salad/)
*Composed dinner salad — substantial enough for Monday salad night. Vegan, packed with protein from chickpeas. Arrange components in sections for visual impact.*


#### Thai Chicken Salad with Chilli Lime Dressing *(Tossed)*

**Cuisine:** Thai
A generously portioned tossed salad with poached and shredded chicken, crunchy shredded cabbage, julienned carrots, fresh mint, cilantro, and crispy fried shallots, all tossed in a tangy-sweet chilli lime dressing with fish sauce depth.

**Key Ingredients:** chicken breast, red cabbage, green cabbage, carrots, cilantro, mint, lime, fish sauce, chilli garlic sauce, peanuts

**Dressing:** Thai chilli lime dressing: 2 tbsp lime juice, 1 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tbsp chilli garlic sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tsp sugar, 1 tbsp vegetable oil. Whisk until sugar dissolves. Adjust lime/fish sauce to taste for the signature sweet-sour-salty-spicy balance.


**Why these flavors work:** The four pillars of Thai flavor — sweet (sugar), sour (lime), salty (fish sauce), and spicy (chilli) — create an electrifying dressing that wakes up raw vegetables. Crunchy cabbage and carrots provide textural contrast to tender chicken, while fresh herbs add aromatic complexity. The peanuts bring fat and crunch, rounding out every bite.


🌿 *Cabbage, carrots, and fresh herbs are widely available in early March. This salad doesn't rely on peak-season produce, making it ideal for early spring.*

**Source:** [RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/thai-chicken-salad/)
*Tossed dinner salad — hearty enough for a full meal. The dressing is mild enough for Elsie (1 chilli rating on the site's scale). Can poach chicken ahead for fast assembly.*

#### Broccoli Salad with Smoky Tamari Almonds
**Cuisine:** American
Crisp raw broccoli florets and diced stems tossed in a tangy, lightly creamy dressing with dried cranberries and sharp red onion, topped with smoked paprika-tamari roasted almonds and pepitas for irresistible crunch.

**Key Ingredients:** broccoli, dried cranberries, red onion, almonds, pepitas, apple cider vinegar, Dijon mustard, olive oil

**Dressing:** Tangy mustard dressing: 2 tbsp mayonnaise, 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar, 2 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp honey, salt and pepper. Whisk until emulsified. The smoky tamari nut topping: toss almonds and pepitas with 1 tbsp tamari, 1/2 tsp maple syrup, 1/4 tsp smoked paprika; toast at 350°F for 12 min.


**Why these flavors work:** Raw broccoli's bitter, vegetal crunch is tamed by the sweet-tart cranberries and the honey in the dressing. The mayo-vinegar dressing is lighter than classic broccoli salad but still coats thoroughly. Smoky tamari nuts replace bacon with a vegetarian umami bomb — same salty-smoky satisfaction without the meat.


🌿 *Broccoli is excellent in early spring on the East Coast — sturdy, affordable, and at its sweetest. This salad improves over 2-3 days in the fridge, making it a perfect prep-ahead side.*

**Source:** [Love & Lemons](https://www.loveandlemons.com/broccoli-salad/)
*Uses up the fresh broccoli supply. Make-ahead side that pairs with almost any dinner this week. Gets better over days — ideal for a busy tech-week schedule.*


#### Fattoush Salad with Sumac-Lemon Vinaigrette
**Cuisine:** Middle Eastern
A vibrant Lebanese bread salad with crispy toasted pita shards, crunchy romaine, juicy tomatoes, cucumber, radishes, and fresh herbs (parsley and mint), tossed in a tangy lemon-sumac vinaigrette with a touch of pomegranate molasses.

**Key Ingredients:** pita bread, romaine lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, radishes, parsley, mint, sumac

**Dressing:** Sumac-lemon vinaigrette: 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice, 1 tsp sumac, 1 tsp pomegranate molasses (optional), 1 small garlic clove (minced), salt and pepper. Whisk together and drizzle over salad just before serving to keep pita crispy.


**Why these flavors work:** Sumac's fruity, citrusy tang is fattoush's defining flavor — it bridges the earthy pita and bright vegetables. Pomegranate molasses adds a sweet-tart depth that elevates a simple salad to something extraordinary. The contrast between crispy pita and cool, juicy vegetables is texturally addictive. This is the Middle Eastern mastery of acid + crunch + herb.


🌿 *Romaine and radishes are solid early-spring choices on the East Coast. Sumac and pomegranate molasses are pantry staples that bring warm-weather brightness to transitional March produce.*

**Source:** [The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/fattoush-salad/)
*Pairs perfectly with shawarma, falafel, or any Middle Eastern dinner this week. The crispy pita makes it fun for Elsie. Quick assembly if pita is toasted ahead.*
