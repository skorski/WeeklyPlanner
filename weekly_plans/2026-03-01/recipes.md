#### Roasted Broccoli, Blood Orange & Burrata Salad *(Composed)*

**Cuisine:** Italian
A composed arrangement of warm roasted broccoli florets, jewel-toned blood orange segments, torn burrata, toasted hazelnuts, and shaved Pecorino. Each element placed with intention on the plate — warm meets cool, creamy meets crunchy, bitter meets sweet.

**Key Ingredients:** broccoli, blood oranges, burrata, hazelnuts, Pecorino Romano, arugula

**Dressing:** Blood orange vinaigrette: fresh blood orange juice, white wine vinegar, Dijon mustard, extra-virgin olive oil, minced shallot, honey, flaky salt, cracked black pepper


**Why these flavors work:** The char on the roasted broccoli provides a nutty bitterness that blood orange's tart sweetness balances perfectly. Burrata adds a lush creaminess that ties the plate together, while hazelnuts deliver crunch and toasty depth. The vinaigrette's acid cuts through the richness — a study in Italian amaro-dolce (bitter-sweet) principles.


🌿 *Blood oranges are at their peak in late winter/early March on the East Coast. Broccoli is a cool-weather champion, at its sweetest now.*

**Source:** [Food52](https://food52.com/recipes/85329-blood-orange-salad-with-burrata)
*Composed dinner salad — substantial enough to be a meal. Stunning presentation. Uses broccoli as a warm element. Seasonal blood oranges at their last gasp of the season.*
