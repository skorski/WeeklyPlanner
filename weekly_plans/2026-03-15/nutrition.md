## Special Notes

- SODIUM STRATEGY: Corned beef appears in 3 dinners (Wed, Thu, Sat) and Sunday gravy has high-sodium sausage. That's 4 high-sodium nights. Rinse the corned beef well before cooking, skip added salt at the table, and lean heavily on potassium-rich foods (bananas, potatoes in Wed dinner, avocado Fri) to help the kidneys flush excess sodium.
- TUESDAY SWIM FUELING: Elsie has swim lessons 6-6:30 PM. Serve the Babo pasta early (5:00-5:15 PM) and keep portions moderate — pasta with butter is ideal pre-exercise fuel (easily digestible carbs). Post-swim, offer a Greek yogurt parfait or cheese stick for protein recovery.

Squeeze half a lemon over the mujadara or serve a small tomato-cucumber side salad to boost absorption by 3-6x.
