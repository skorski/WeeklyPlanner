#### Dinner: Pasta and Gravy (Sunday Gravy)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Italian-American |
| **Key Ingredients** | San Marzano tomatoes, pork ribs, Italian sausage, beef, garlic, basil, pasta |
| **Source** | [Recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/italian-american-red-sauce-recipe) |
Italian-American Sunday Gravy with pork ribs, sausage, and beef in slow-simmered tomato sauce over pasta







##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** You brown every piece of meat like your life depends on it, because it does. Season the sausage, the ribs, the beef with salt and pepper, then sear them in olive oil until they're singing and dark on every side. Pull them out, soften your onions and garlic in those beautiful drippings, stir in the tomato paste until it goes brick red, then hit it with wine and let it hiss. Pour in your San Marzanos, crush them with your hands like God intended, add oregano, basil, a bay leaf, and a whisper of red pepper flakes. Nestle the meats back in, bring it to a lazy bubble, and let it go for three, four hours, stirring every half hour so nothing sticks. Anyone who uses jarred sauce on a Sunday should be ashamed of themselves. Add your meatballs in the last hour so they soak up glory without falling apart. Serve over rigatoni with a mountain of Parmigiano and all your people around the table.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — Season meats, brown in batches in oil until deeply colored on all sides. Set aside.**
- 1 lb Italian sausage (sweet or hot) *(links)*
- 1 lb pork ribs *(country-style, separated)*
- 1 lb beef chuck *(cut into 2-inch pieces)*
- 2 tbsp olive oil
-  salt and black pepper

**Step 2 — Saute onion in drippings 5 min, add garlic 1 min, stir in paste until brick-red, deglaze with wine.**
- 1 large yellow onion *(diced)*
- 4 cloves garlic *(minced)*
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 cup dry red wine

**Step 3 — Add tomatoes, puree, herbs, and spices. Return browned meats to pot.**
- 2 (28 oz) cans whole San Marzano tomatoes *(hand-crushed)*
- 1 (28 oz) can tomato puree
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp fresh basil *(torn)*
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- pinch sugar *(optional, to balance acidity)*

**Final:** Bring to a simmer, partially cover, cook 3-4 hours stirring every 30 min → Add meatballs in last 60 min → Remove bay leaves, adjust seasoning → Toss with cooked rigatoni, top with grated Parmigiano-Reggiano

**Variations:**
- **Slow-Cooker Sunday Gravy** — Brown the meats on the stovetop, then dump everything into a slow cooker for 8 hours of hands-off simmering. ([recipe](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/food-network-kitchen/slow-cooker-sunday-gravy-3362170))
- **Pressure Cooker Sunday Gravy** — Achieve the same depth of flavor in 45 minutes at high pressure, perfect for when Sunday sneaks up on you. ([recipe](https://afeastfortheeyes.net/2015/05/sunday-gravy-pressure-cooker-style.html))
- **Nana's Braciole Sunday Gravy** — Thin-pounded beef rolls stuffed with breadcrumbs, garlic, Pecorino, and parsley braise in the sauce for old-world elegance. ([recipe](https://www.platingsandpairings.com/authentic-italian-sunday-gravy/))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** Parmesan Rind Broth Bomb — Drop a 3-inch rind of Parmigiano-Reggiano into the sauce after your tomatoes go in. It melts slowly over 3-4 hours, releasing glutamates that deepen the sauce beyond what meat alone can do. Fish out the softened rind before serving — or don't, it's edible and delicious.
- **PREPARATION:** Tomato Paste Brick — Before building your sauce, clear a spot in your Dutch oven and press 3 tablespoons of tomato paste flat against the bare metal. Let it cook undisturbed for 2-3 minutes until it turns from bright red to a deep brick color and smells almost smoky. Then stir it into your sautéed onions and garlic before deglazing with a generous pour of dry red wine.
- **SAUCE:** Finishing Oil with Calabrian Chili — In the last 10 minutes of simmering, stir in 1 tablespoon of finely minced Calabrian chili peppers (from a jar). When plating, finish each bowl with a thread of your best extra-virgin olive oil — something grassy and peppery — right over the dressed pasta.
- **TEXTURE:** Torn Garlic Bread Croutons — Take day-old Italian bread, tear it into rough 1-inch pieces — never cut, tearing creates more surface area. Toss with olive oil, granulated garlic, and flaky salt. Bake at 400°F for 8-10 minutes until golden and craggy. Scatter 5-6 pieces over each bowl of pasta.

#### Dinner: Mujadara (Lentils and Rice with Crispy Onions)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Middle Eastern (Levantine) |
| **Key Ingredients** | green lentils, basmati rice, yellow onions, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, yogurt |
| **Source** | [Recipe](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/mujadara-lentils-and-rice/) |
Spiced green lentils and basmati rice topped with crispy fried onions, served with herbed yogurt







##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** Rinse your black lentils and get them into a pot with 2 cups of water, bring it to a boil, then simmer covered for 10 minutes so they're just par-cooked, not mush. Drain them and set aside. Now, the onions: dice two big yellow onions and cook them in a quarter cup of good olive oil over medium-high heat until they go dark, dark golden brown, darker than you think, about 40 minutes with a teaspoon of salt. Pour in the remaining 2 cups of water, stir in the rinsed and soaked rice, those par-cooked lentils, a half teaspoon of cumin, salt and pepper, then cover and cook low for 20 minutes until everything is tender and the liquid is gone. While that cooks, slice a third onion into thin rings, fry them in olive oil until shatteringly crispy and deep brown, then drain on paper towels. Let the pot rest 10 minutes, then pile it in bowls, crown it with those crispy onions, and serve with yogurt and a bright salad.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — Boil, then simmer covered 10-12 min until par-cooked. Drain and set aside.**
- 1 cup black lentils *(sorted and rinsed)*
- 2 cups water *(for par-cooking)*

**Step 2 — Cook onions in oil over medium-high heat, stirring, until very dark golden brown, about 40 min.**
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 large yellow onions *(diced (about 4 cups))*
- 1 tsp kosher salt

**Step 3 — Add water to caramelized onions, bring to boil. Stir in rice, lentils, cumin, salt, pepper. Cover, simmer low 15-20 min.**
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup long-grain white rice *(soaked 15 min, drained)*
- 1/2 - 1 tsp cumin
-  black pepper *(to taste)*

**Final:** Let rest covered 10 min → Meanwhile, fry 1 thinly sliced onion in 2-3 tbsp olive oil until crispy and deeply golden → Transfer to bowls, top with crispy onions and parsley → Serve with plain yogurt and a fresh salad

**Variations:**
- **Mujadara Hamra (Red Mujadara)** — Swap rice for coarse bulgur wheat and caramelize the onions until nearly burnt for a deep reddish-brown, chewy, pilaf-style dish. ([recipe](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/recipe/mujadara-hamra/))
- **Syrian One-Pot Mujadara with Bulgur** — Cook lentils and bulgur together in one pot with cucumber-mint yogurt on the side for a quick weeknight version. ([recipe](https://zenandzaatar.com/one-pot-mujadara-syrian-bulgur-and-lentils-with-cucumber-mint-yogurt-vegan/))
- **Jordanian-Style Mujadara Pilaf** — Uses green lentils and short-grain rice for a fluffy, pilaf-like texture with warm spices like allspice and cinnamon. ([recipe](https://littlesunnykitchen.com/mujadara-with-bulgur/))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** Bloom the Spice Trinity in Rendered Onion Fat — After frying your crispy onion topping, reserve 3 tablespoons of that onion-infused frying oil. In a separate pot, heat the reserved oil until it shimmers, then add your cumin seeds, coriander seeds, and a cracked cinnamon stick. Let them pop and sizzle for 60-90 seconds until the cumin turns a shade darker and you can smell it across the room. Toast your rice directly in this bloomed spice oil for 2 minutes before adding your par-cooked lentils and stock.
- **SAUCE:** Herbed Yogurt with Lemon Zest — Mix 1 cup of full-fat plain yogurt with 2 tablespoons of finely chopped fresh mint, 1 tablespoon of chopped fresh dill, the zest of one lemon, a pinch of sumac if you have it, and a generous crack of black pepper. Let it sit for 15 minutes before serving. Spoon it alongside the mujadara — never on top, let people decide their own ratio.
- **TEXTURE:** Shatteringly Crispy Two-Stage Onions — Slice 3 large yellow onions into paper-thin rings using a mandoline. Toss with 1 tablespoon of cornstarch and a pinch of salt. Fry in 350°F vegetable oil in batches — do not crowd the pot — stirring gently until they turn deep amber, about 8-10 minutes. Pull them slightly before you think they're done; they'll darken as they drain. Cool on a wire rack, not paper towels — airflow keeps them shatteringly crisp.

#### Dinner: Babo Pasta (Homemade Spinach Pasta)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Italian |
| **Key Ingredients** | 00 flour, egg yolks, fresh spinach, Parmigiano-Reggiano, butter, garlic |
| **Source** | [Recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/fresh-pasta-recipe) |
The family signature dish: silky fresh pasta with spinach puree and golden egg yolks, brown butter and Parmesan







##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** Mound your flour on a clean board, make a well deep enough to baptize a baby in, and crack your yolks and whole egg right into the center. Add the spinach puree and a tiny drizzle of olive oil. With a fork, pull flour in from the inner walls, gently, gently, until it thickens into a shaggy mess you can touch with your hands. Now knead, 10 full minutes, pushing with the heel of your palm, folding, turning, until the dough is smooth as silk and springs back when you poke it. Wrap it tight in plastic and let it rest 30 minutes because patience is a virtue and gluten needs to relax. Roll through the pasta machine starting at the widest setting, fold it over itself twice to build structure, then thin it down to the second-to-last setting until you can almost see your hand through it. Cut into tagliatelle, dust with semolina, and boil in very salty water for barely 2 minutes. Toss with browned butter and the extra yolks, or whatever your heart tells you.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — Blanch, ice bath, squeeze bone-dry, puree until smooth. Set aside.**
- 2 cups (60g) fresh spinach *(blanched 30 sec, squeezed dry, pureed smooth)*

**Step 2 — Pour yolks, egg, spinach puree, oil into flour well. Fork-mix from center outward, then knead 10 min until smooth.**
- 300g (2 1/3 cups) 00 flour *(mounded on board with deep well)*
- 5 egg yolks
- 1 whole egg
- 2 tbsp spinach puree *(from Group A)*
- 1 tsp olive oil *(optional)*
- pinch fine salt

**Step 3 — Roll rested dough through pasta machine to second-thinnest setting, cut into tagliatelle, dust with semolina.**
-  semolina flour *(for dusting)*
-  heavily salted water *(at a rolling boil)*

**Final:** Wrap dough in plastic, rest 30 min at room temperature → Roll, fold twice for structure, then thin progressively → Cut into tagliatelle, dust with semolina → Boil in heavily salted water for 90 seconds to 2 min → Toss with browned butter, reserved yolks, or your sauce of choice

**Variations:**
- **Classic Golden Egg Yolk Pasta** — Skip the spinach and use 7 egg yolks plus 1 whole egg for an intensely rich, golden-yellow pasta with silky chew. ([recipe](https://mosthungry.com/egg-yolk-pasta-dough/))
- **Squid Ink Pasta** — Add 1 tablespoon squid ink to the egg mixture for dramatic black noodles with a subtle briny flavor, perfect with shellfish. ([recipe](https://www.pestopasteria.com/post/pasta-school-chapter-4-advanced-pasta-dough-techniques))
- **Saffron Pasta Dough** — Steep a pinch of saffron threads in hot water, cool, then mix into the eggs for golden, aromatic noodles. ([recipe](https://www.the-pasta-project.com/how-to-make-colored-pasta-at-home/))

##### Chef's Tips
- **SAUCE:** Brown Butter with Crispy Sage — Cut 6 tablespoons of unsalted butter into a light-colored stainless pan — never nonstick, you need to see the color change. Cook over medium heat, swirling constantly, until the milk solids turn the color of a hazelnut shell and the kitchen smells like toasting nuts, about 4-5 minutes. The moment it hits that color, throw in 12-15 fresh sage leaves. They'll sputter violently for 10 seconds and then go crisp and translucent. Pull the pan off heat immediately. Toss your drained pasta directly into this pan with 2 tablespoons of reserved pasta water.
- **PREPARATION:** Squeeze the Spinach Until It Begs — After blanching your spinach for exactly 30 seconds in boiling salted water and shocking it in ice water, wring it out in a clean kitchen towel until not a single drop of moisture remains. Then — and this is critical — chop it fine on a cutting board, gather it again into the towel, and wring it a second time. You want it as dry as a puck of compressed leaves.
- **ACID:** Lemon Zest Snow at the Table — Using a Microplane, zest one lemon directly over each plated bowl at the table, right before anyone picks up a fork. Let the fine zest fall like snow over the brown butter and sage. Follow with a light grating of aged Parmigiano-Reggiano and a few cracks of black pepper.
- **TEXTURE:** Toasted Pine Nut Scatter — In a dry skillet over medium-low heat — patience, not fire — toast 3 tablespoons of pine nuts, tossing every 30 seconds until they're spotted golden and fragrant, about 3-4 minutes. Pull them off heat immediately; they go from toasted to burnt in 15 seconds. Scatter over the plated pasta.

#### Dinner: Corned Beef and Cabbage with Potatoes and Carrots

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Irish-American |
| **Key Ingredients** | corned beef brisket, green cabbage, Yukon Gold potatoes, carrots, bay leaves, mustard seed |
| **Source** | [Recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/corned-beef-and-cabbage-recipe) |
Salt-cured brisket slow-braised with cabbage wedges, waxy potatoes, and sweet carrots. Served with whole-grain mustard.







##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** Take that beautiful brisket out of the package, rinse it under cold water to wash off the excess brine, and set it fat-side up in your biggest pot. Cover it with cold water by a couple of inches, toss in the spice packet (or your own peppercorns, mustard seeds, bay leaves, and garlic), and bring it to a boil. Skim the gray foam off the top, because nobody wants to eat that. Drop the heat to a gentle simmer, not a boil, cover the pot, and let it go for about 3 hours until a fork slides in and out like butter. Add your halved potatoes and chunked carrots with 30 minutes to go, and your cabbage wedges with 15 minutes left so they stay tender but not sad. Pull the brisket out, let it rest 10 minutes, then slice it thin against the grain. Pile it on a platter with the vegetables, ladle a little cooking broth over everything, and put the mustard and horseradish on the table where they belong.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — Place brisket in large pot, cover with cold water, add spices. Bring to boil, skim foam, reduce to gentle simmer.**
- 4-5 lb corned beef brisket *(rinsed, fat-side up)*
- 1 spice packet *(included with brisket (or DIY: peppercorns, mustard seed, bay leaves, garlic))*
-  cold water *(enough to cover by 2 inches)*

**Step 2 — Add to pot after brisket has simmered 2.5 hours. Cook 15 min.**
- 2 lbs small potatoes *(halved or quartered)*
- 1 lb carrots *(peeled, cut into 2-inch pieces)*
- 1 large yellow onion *(cut into wedges)*

**Step 3 — Add cabbage wedges to pot, cook another 15-20 min until tender.**
- 1 head green cabbage *(cut into 6-8 wedges)*

**Final:** Simmer brisket covered 3 hours total until fork-tender → Add potatoes, carrots, onion at 2.5 hours → Add cabbage at 2 hours 45 min → Rest brisket 10 min, slice thin against the grain → Arrange on platter with vegetables, ladle broth over, serve with mustard and horseradish

**Variations:**
- **Instant Pot Corned Beef & Cabbage** — Pressure-cook the brisket for 90 minutes at high pressure for fork-tender results in half the time. ([recipe](https://stripedspatula.com/instant-pot-corned-beef/))
- **Beer-Braised Oven Corned Beef** — Braise in a Dutch oven at 300F with Guinness stout for 3 hours, then glaze with mustard and brown sugar under the broiler. ([recipe](https://supersafeway.com/the-best-beer-braised-instant-pot-corned-beef-cabbage/))
- **Slow Cooker Corned Beef** — Layer vegetables on the bottom, brisket on top, set it on low for 8 hours and walk away completely. ([recipe](https://selfproclaimedfoodie.com/pressure-cooker-corned-beef-cabbage/))

##### Chef's Tips
- **SAUCE:** Horseradish Cream with Chives — Combine 1/2 cup sour cream with 3 tablespoons of prepared horseradish (not the creamy kind — the stuff that makes your eyes water), 1 teaspoon of Dijon mustard, 1 tablespoon of minced fresh chives, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Thin with 1 tablespoon of the braising liquid if needed. Serve cold, directly alongside hot sliced beef.
- **PREPARATION:** Stagger Your Vegetables Like a Chef — Stop cooking everything together from the start. Pull the corned beef when fork-tender (about 3 hours). Add halved Yukon Golds and thick-cut carrots to the simmering braising liquid and cook 25 minutes. Add cabbage wedges — cut through the core so they hold together — for only the last 12 minutes. Rest the beef tented in foil for 15 minutes. Slice against the grain, always against the grain.
- **ACID:** Whole-Grain Mustard Vinaigrette for the Vegetables — Whisk together 2 tablespoons of whole-grain mustard, 1 tablespoon of apple cider vinegar, 3 tablespoons of extra-virgin olive oil, 1 teaspoon of honey, and a pinch of salt. Spoon this warm vinaigrette over the plated potatoes, carrots, and cabbage right before serving.

#### Dinner: Corned Beef Tacos with Pickled Onions and Mustard Crema

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Irish-Mexican Fusion |
| **Key Ingredients** | leftover corned beef, flour tortillas, red onion, cabbage, sour cream, mustard, lime, cilantro |
| **Source** | [Recipe](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/corned-beef-tacos) |
Shredded leftover corned beef in flour tortillas with quick-pickled red onions, shredded cabbage, and bright mustard crema







##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** Start the pickled onions first because they need time to turn pink and gorgeous: slice a red onion thin, heat vinegar, water, sugar, and salt until dissolved, pour it over the onions in a jar, and walk away for 30 minutes. Mix your mustard crema next: sour cream, spicy brown mustard, a squeeze of lime, and if you're feeling brave, a spoonful of horseradish. Now take your leftover corned beef, shred it with two forks or chop it rough, and get it into a hot skillet with a little oil until the edges go crispy and golden, about 5 to 7 minutes. Warm your tortillas in a dry skillet until they puff and get those little brown spots. Load each one up with that crispy beef, a tangle of shredded cabbage, pickled onions, avocado slices, and a generous drizzle of the crema. Finish with cilantro and a squeeze of lime, and try to eat just one.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — Heat vinegar, water, sugar, salt until dissolved. Pour over onion slices, let sit 30 min.**
- 1/2 red onion *(thinly sliced)*
- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

**Step 2 — Whisk together until smooth. Season with a pinch of salt. Refrigerate.**
- 1/2 cup sour cream or Mexican crema
- 3 tbsp spicy brown mustard
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp prepared horseradish *(optional)*

**Step 3 — Heat oil in skillet over medium-high. Cook corned beef until edges are crispy, 5-7 min.**
- 2 cups cooked corned beef *(shredded or chopped (leftover from Wednesday))*
- 1-2 tbsp vegetable oil

**Step 4 — Warm tortillas, assemble with all components.**
- 8 small flour or corn tortillas *(warmed in dry skillet)*
- 1 cup green cabbage *(shredded)*
- 1 avocado *(sliced)*
-  fresh cilantro *(for garnish)*
- 1 lime *(cut into wedges)*

**Final:** Layer crispy corned beef on warm tortillas → Top with shredded cabbage, pickled onions, avocado slices → Drizzle mustard crema generously → Finish with cilantro and a squeeze of lime

**Variations:**
- **Reuben Tacos** — Swap pickled onions and crema for sauerkraut, melted Swiss cheese, and Russian dressing for a deli-meets-taqueria mashup. ([recipe](https://40aprons.com/reuben-tacos/))
- **Korean-Fusion Corned Beef Tacos** — Toss the corned beef with gochujang and sesame oil, top with kimchi slaw and spicy mayo for a Korean street-food spin. ([recipe](https://www.tasteofhome.com/recipes/spicy-corned-beef-tacos/))
- **Creamy Mustard & Microgreen Tacos** — Add peppery microgreens and a mustard-mayo sauce with a touch of horseradish for a lighter, more refined taco. ([recipe](https://www.runningtothekitchen.com/corned-beef-tacos/))

##### Chef's Tips
- **TEXTURE:** Griddle-Crisp the Corned Beef — Shred leftover corned beef by hand — not chopped, shredded, so you get thin irregular strands with maximum surface area. Heat a cast-iron skillet screaming hot with a thin slick of canola oil, just past the smoke point. Spread the shredded beef in a single layer and do not touch it for 3 full minutes. You want dark, craggy, almost-burnt edges. Flip once, press flat with a spatula, and crisp the other side for 2 minutes. Hit it with a squeeze of lime juice right off the heat.
- **SAUCE:** Double-Mustard Crema with Dill — Stir together 1/2 cup sour cream, 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard, 1 tablespoon whole-grain mustard, the juice of half a lime, 1 tablespoon of finely chopped fresh dill, and a pinch of smoked paprika. Let it sit in the refrigerator for at least 20 minutes before serving so the flavors marry.
- **ACID:** 15-Minute Pickled Onions with Allspice — Slice 1 large red onion into paper-thin rings. Pack into a jar. Bring 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar, 1/2 cup water, 1 tablespoon sugar, 1 teaspoon salt, 3 allspice berries, and 1 bay leaf to a boil. Pour over the onions and let sit at least 15 minutes. They'll turn electric magenta and taste like sharp, sweet perfume.
- **PREPARATION:** Charred Flour Tortillas Over Open Flame — If you have a gas stove, hold each flour tortilla directly over the burner flame with tongs for 10-15 seconds per side until you see black char spots and the tortilla puffs in places. If electric, use a bone-dry cast-iron pan at maximum heat. Wrap charred tortillas in a clean kitchen towel to steam and stay pliable.

#### Dinner: Big Dinner Salad (Vegetarian Night)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | American |
| **Key Ingredients** | mixed greens, chickpeas, avocado, soft-boiled eggs, Parmesan, pepitas, pickled beets, lemon, tahini |
| **Source** | [Recipe](https://cookieandkate.com/big-dinner-salad/) |
Composed dinner salad with roasted chickpeas, avocado, soft-boiled eggs, Parmesan, pepitas, pickled beets, and lemon-tahini dressing







##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** This is not a sad little side salad, this is dinner, so treat it with respect. Drain and rinse your chickpeas, pat them dry, and if you want them extra good, toss them in olive oil with cumin and roast at 400F for 20 minutes until they crackle when you bite them. Boil your eggs for exactly 7 minutes if you want the yolks jammy, then shock them in ice water and peel. Whisk your tahini dressing until it's creamy and pourable: tahini, lemon juice, garlic, a drizzle of olive oil, a touch of maple syrup, and enough water to thin it out. Now build your masterpiece on a big platter: pile up your greens, scatter the chickpeas, arrange the beet wedges and avocado slices, nestle in the halved eggs, and shower it all with the dressing, herbs, and a handful of pepitas. Every bite should have something different happening.


**How It Comes Together**
*Preheat oven to 400F (for optional roasted chickpeas)*

**Step 1 — Whisk tahini, lemon juice, garlic, oil, maple syrup. Add water until pourable. Season with salt.**
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 3 tbsp lemon juice *(about 1 lemon)*
- 1 clove garlic *(minced)*
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tsp maple syrup
- 2-3 tbsp water *(to thin)*
- pinch salt

**Step 2 — Toss chickpeas with oil, cumin, salt. Roast at 400F for 20 min until crispy (or use straight from the can).**
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas *(drained, rinsed, patted dry)*
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- pinch salt

**Step 3 — Prep eggs, beets, avocado. Arrange greens on a large platter.**
- 2 eggs *(boiled 7 min for jammy yolks, ice-bathed, peeled, halved)*
- 2 small cooked beets *(cut into wedges (pre-cooked or roasted))*
- 1 ripe avocado *(sliced)*
- 4-5 cups mixed greens *(arugula, spinach, romaine)*
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes *(halved)*
- 2 tbsp fresh herbs *(parsley or dill, chopped)*
- 2 tbsp pepitas or sunflower seeds

**Final:** Spread mixed greens on a large platter or in bowls → Arrange chickpeas, beet wedges, avocado slices, and halved eggs on top → Scatter tomatoes, herbs, and seeds → Drizzle generously with lemon-tahini dressing → Serve immediately while the avocado is perfect

**Variations:**
- **Mediterranean Power Bowl** — Add quinoa, feta, olives, and roasted red peppers to transform it into a hearty grain-based power bowl. ([recipe](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/recipes/mediterranean-grain-bowl))
- **Warm Roasted Vegetable Dinner Salad** — Roast the beets, sweet potato, and chickpeas together, serve warm over greens with a warm lemon-tahini dressing. ([recipe](https://blessthismeal.com/the-best-mediterranean-power-bowls-recipe/))
- **Vegan Protein Salad with Tofu** — Replace eggs with crispy pan-seared tofu and add marinated white beans for a fully plant-based protein punch. ([recipe](https://www.threelittlechickpeas.com/mediterranean-power-bowl/))

##### Chef's Tips
- **TEXTURE:** Crispy Spiced Chickpeas — Drain and rinse a can of chickpeas. Pat them aggressively dry with paper towels — roll them around, pop off any loose skins. Toss with 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1 teaspoon cumin, 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, and a generous pinch of salt. Spread on a sheet pan in a single layer and roast at 425°F for 25-30 minutes, shaking the pan once at the halfway mark. They should rattle when you shake the pan — that's how you know they're done.
- **SAUCE:** Lemon-Tahini Dressing with Raw Garlic — In a jar, combine 1/4 cup tahini, the juice of 1 lemon, 1 small clove of garlic grated on a Microplane, 2 tablespoons cold water, 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon cumin, and a pinch of salt. Shake violently until emulsified and pourable — it should be the consistency of heavy cream. If it seizes up (tahini does this), add more water, 1 tablespoon at a time, and keep shaking.
- **TEMPERATURE:** 6.5-Minute Soft-Boiled Eggs — Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil. Lower eggs straight from the refrigerator into the water with a slotted spoon. Set a timer for exactly 6 minutes and 30 seconds. When the timer goes off, transfer eggs immediately to an ice bath for 5 minutes. Peel under cool running water. Halve just before plating — the yolk should be molten gold, not chalky, not raw.
- **ACID:** Quick-Pickled Shallots — Slice 2 shallots into thin rings. Submerge in a mixture of 1/3 cup rice vinegar, 1 tablespoon sugar, and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Let sit at least 20 minutes. Scatter over the composed salad alongside the pickled beets for a double-pickle punch.

#### Dinner: Corned Beef Kimchi Fried Rice Bowls

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cuisine** | Korean Fusion |
| **Key Ingredients** | leftover corned beef, day-old rice, kimchi, gochujang, sesame oil, eggs, scallions |
| **Source** | [Recipe](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/263929/corned-beef-kimchi-fried-rice/) |
Day-old rice stir-fried with leftover corned beef, kimchi, gochujang, and sesame oil, topped with a fried egg







##### Recipe Directions

> **Mamma Karen Says:** Use yesterday's rice, cold from the fridge, because fresh rice makes fried rice into sad porridge and I will not have it. Drain your kimchi over a bowl and save every drop of that juice because it's liquid gold. Heat oil in a big nonstick skillet, get your chopped corned beef in there and cook it until it starts to crisp on the edges. Chop the kimchi into small pieces, toss it into the pan, and let it all sizzle and get friendly for about 5 minutes. Now dump in the cold rice, the reserved kimchi juice, a splash of water, and a good spoonful of gochujang, then spread it flat across the pan and leave it alone for 3 minutes so the bottom gets crunchy. Stir it up, press it down again, and keep doing that for about 12 minutes until you've got those gorgeous crispy bits throughout. Finish with green onions, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of sesame oil, then crown each bowl with a fried egg with crispy edges and a runny yolk.


**How It Comes Together**


**Step 1 — Heat oil in large nonstick skillet. Add corned beef, cook until edges crisp. Add chopped kimchi, cook 4-5 min until browned.**
- 1 1/4 cups kimchi *(drained, juice reserved, chopped)*
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups cooked corned beef *(diced (leftover from Wednesday))*

**Step 2 — Add rice, water, kimchi juice, and gochujang to skillet. Spread flat, cook 3-4 min, stir and press. Repeat for 12-15 min total until crusty.**
- 3 cups cold cooked rice *(day-old, from fridge)*
- 1/4 cup water
-  reserved kimchi juice
- 2 tbsp gochujang *(Korean hot pepper paste)*

**Step 3 — Stir in green onions, sesame seeds, sesame oil, soy sauce. Fry eggs separately in a hot skillet.**
- 1/2 cup green onions *(sliced)*
- 2 tsp toasted sesame seeds
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1/4 tsp soy sauce *(to taste)*
- 4 eggs *(fried with crispy edges and runny yolks)*

**Final:** Divide fried rice among 4 bowls → Top each bowl with a fried egg → Garnish with extra green onion and sesame seeds → Serve immediately while the rice is still crackling

**Variations:**
- **Spam Kimchi Fried Rice** — Replace corned beef with diced Spam for a Hawaiian-Korean comfort classic with extra salty crunch. ([recipe](https://www.spam.com/recipes/spam-kimchi-fried-rice))
- **Bacon Kimchi Fried Rice** — Render chopped bacon until crispy, cook everything in the smoky bacon fat for deeply savory flavor. ([recipe](https://onolicioushawaii.com/kimchi-fried-rice/))
- **Vegan Tofu Kimchi Fried Rice** — Use crispy pan-seared tofu cubes and vegan kimchi, finish with a tofu scramble instead of a fried egg. ([recipe](https://www.downtoearth.org/recipes/appetizers-and-sides/kim-chi-spam-fried-rice))

##### Chef's Tips
- **PREPARATION:** Caramelize the Kimchi First — Before anything else touches the wok, add your chopped kimchi (use the funky, over-fermented stuff from the back of the jar — that's the good kimchi) to a dry, screaming-hot carbon steel wok or cast-iron skillet. No oil yet. Let it sear and char for 3-4 minutes, stirring only once, until the edges darken and the liquid evaporates. Then push to the side and add your sesame oil for the rice.
- **TEXTURE:** Socarrat-Style Crispy Rice Bottom — Once your fried rice is mixed and seasoned, press it firmly into the wok with the back of a spatula. Reduce heat to medium and let it sit completely undisturbed for 3-4 minutes. You'll hear it crackling — that's the rice forming a golden crust against the metal. When you scoop portions, make sure each bowl gets some of that crispy bottom layer. This is the socarrat. This is the prize.
- **SAUCE:** Gochujang-Sesame Drizzle Sauce — Whisk together 2 tablespoons gochujang, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil, 1 teaspoon honey, and 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger. Thin with 1 tablespoon of water until it's a pourable consistency. Finish each bowl with a thread of this sauce in a zig-zag pattern over the fried egg.
- **ACID:** Scallion-Sesame Salad Garnish — Slice 4-5 scallions on a sharp bias into 2-inch pieces. Toss with 1 teaspoon of toasted sesame oil, 1 teaspoon of rice vinegar, a pinch of gochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes), and a sprinkle of toasted sesame seeds. Mound this raw, bright salad on top of each hot bowl just before serving. ## Nutrition Summary

This week's menu is March comfort cooking at its best — hearty, warm, and built around a brilliantly efficient corned beef that feeds three dinners. The protein diversity is genuinely excellent, with ten distinct sources from pork ribs to lentils to pepitas. Iron coverage is a standout: Laura and Elsie get heme iron from corned beef in three dinners, plant iron from Monday's lentils and Friday's chickpeas, and the vitamin C in cabbage, lime, and lemon helps absorb it all. Friday's big dinner salad is the nutritional MVP of the week — fiber, healthy fats, plant protein, vitamin E, magnesium, and potassium all in one bowl. The main watchpoint is sodium: corned beef is salt-cured by nature, and having it three nights means sodium runs high mid-to-late week. Counter that with potassium-rich bananas and avocado, and keep lunches on the lighter side. The omega-3 and vitamin D gaps are typical for any no-fish week — tuna or salmon lunches plus a D3 supplement close them cleanly. With the suggested lunches (especially the tuna salad and salmon onigiri for omega-3, and the PB&J and wraps for whole grains) and snacks (banana-walnut for potassium, yogurt parfait for calcium, trail mix for vitamin E), this family hits adequate intake across every major nutrient category. The week leans rich, which is exactly right for mid-March. Add some lighter lunches to keep energy steady, and you're golden.
