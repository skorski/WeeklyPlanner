## Sunday — Pasta & Gravy (Sunday Gravy)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/italian-american-red-sauce-recipe) |
| **Servings** | 6-8 |
| **Prep** | 30 min |
| **Cook** | 4 hours |

### Nonna Says

> You brown every piece of meat like your life depends on it, because it does. Season the sausage, the ribs, the beef with salt and pepper, then sear them in olive oil until they're singing and dark on every side. Pull them out, soften your onions and garlic in those beautiful drippings, stir in the tomato paste until it goes brick red, then hit it with wine and let it hiss. Pour in your San Marzanos, crush them with your hands like God intended, add oregano, basil, a bay leaf, and a whisper of red pepper flakes. Nestle the meats back in, bring it to a lazy bubble, and let it go for three, four hours, stirring every half hour so nothing sticks. Anyone who uses jarred sauce on a Sunday should be ashamed of themselves. Add your meatballs in the last hour so they soak up glory without falling apart. Serve over rigatoni with a mountain of Parmigiano and all your people around the table.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → Season meats, brown in batches in oil until deeply colored on all sides. Set aside.**

- 1 lb Italian sausage (sweet or hot) *(links)*

- 1 lb pork ribs *(country-style, separated)*

- 1 lb beef chuck *(cut into 2-inch pieces)*

- 2 tbsp olive oil

-  salt and black pepper


**Group B → Saute onion in drippings 5 min, add garlic 1 min, stir in paste until brick-red, deglaze with wine.**

- 1 large yellow onion *(diced)*

- 4 cloves garlic *(minced)*

- 2 tbsp tomato paste

- 1/2 cup dry red wine


**Group C → Add tomatoes, puree, herbs, and spices. Return browned meats to pot.**

- 2 (28 oz) cans whole San Marzano tomatoes *(hand-crushed)*

- 1 (28 oz) can tomato puree

- 1 tsp dried oregano

- 1 tbsp fresh basil *(torn)*

- 2 bay leaves

- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes

- pinch sugar *(optional, to balance acidity)*


**Final assembly:** Bring to a simmer, partially cover, cook 3-4 hours stirring every 30 min → Add meatballs in last 60 min → Remove bay leaves, adjust seasoning → Toss with cooked rigatoni, top with grated Parmigiano-Reggiano

### Variations

1. **Slow-Cooker Sunday Gravy** — Brown the meats on the stovetop, then dump everything into a slow cooker for 8 hours of hands-off simmering. ([recipe](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/food-network-kitchen/slow-cooker-sunday-gravy-3362170))

2. **Pressure Cooker Sunday Gravy** — Achieve the same depth of flavor in 45 minutes at high pressure, perfect for when Sunday sneaks up on you. ([recipe](https://afeastfortheeyes.net/2015/05/sunday-gravy-pressure-cooker-style.html))

3. **Nana's Braciole Sunday Gravy** — Thin-pounded beef rolls stuffed with breadcrumbs, garlic, Pecorino, and parsley braise in the sauce for old-world elegance. ([recipe](https://www.platingsandpairings.com/authentic-italian-sunday-gravy/))

## Monday — Mujadara (Lentils & Rice with Crispy Onions)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [The Mediterranean Dish](https://www.themediterraneandish.com/mujadara-lentils-and-rice/) |
| **Servings** | 4-6 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 1 hour |

### Nonna Says

> Rinse your black lentils and get them into a pot with 2 cups of water, bring it to a boil, then simmer covered for 10 minutes so they're just par-cooked, not mush. Drain them and set aside. Now, the onions: dice two big yellow onions and cook them in a quarter cup of good olive oil over medium-high heat until they go dark, dark golden brown, darker than you think, about 40 minutes with a teaspoon of salt. Pour in the remaining 2 cups of water, stir in the rinsed and soaked rice, those par-cooked lentils, a half teaspoon of cumin, salt and pepper, then cover and cook low for 20 minutes until everything is tender and the liquid is gone. While that cooks, slice a third onion into thin rings, fry them in olive oil until shatteringly crispy and deep brown, then drain on paper towels. Let the pot rest 10 minutes, then pile it in bowls, crown it with those crispy onions, and serve with yogurt and a bright salad.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → Boil, then simmer covered 10-12 min until par-cooked. Drain and set aside.**

- 1 cup black lentils *(sorted and rinsed)*

- 2 cups water *(for par-cooking)*


**Group B → Cook onions in oil over medium-high heat, stirring, until very dark golden brown, about 40 min.**

- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil

- 2 large yellow onions *(diced (about 4 cups))*

- 1 tsp kosher salt


**Group C → Add water to caramelized onions, bring to boil. Stir in rice, lentils, cumin, salt, pepper. Cover, simmer low 15-20 min.**

- 2 cups water

- 1 cup long-grain white rice *(soaked 15 min, drained)*

- 1/2 - 1 tsp cumin

-  black pepper *(to taste)*


**Final assembly:** Let rest covered 10 min → Meanwhile, fry 1 thinly sliced onion in 2-3 tbsp olive oil until crispy and deeply golden → Transfer to bowls, top with crispy onions and parsley → Serve with plain yogurt and a fresh salad

### Variations

1. **Mujadara Hamra (Red Mujadara)** — Swap rice for coarse bulgur wheat and caramelize the onions until nearly burnt for a deep reddish-brown, chewy, pilaf-style dish. ([recipe](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/recipe/mujadara-hamra/))

2. **Syrian One-Pot Mujadara with Bulgur** — Cook lentils and bulgur together in one pot with cucumber-mint yogurt on the side for a quick weeknight version. ([recipe](https://zenandzaatar.com/one-pot-mujadara-syrian-bulgur-and-lentils-with-cucumber-mint-yogurt-vegan/))

3. **Jordanian-Style Mujadara Pilaf** — Uses green lentils and short-grain rice for a fluffy, pilaf-like texture with warm spices like allspice and cinnamon. ([recipe](https://littlesunnykitchen.com/mujadara-with-bulgur/))

## Tuesday — Babo Pasta (Homemade Spinach Egg Yolk Pasta)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Serious Eats (family adaptation)](https://www.seriouseats.com/fresh-pasta-recipe) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 45 min (plus 30 min rest) |
| **Cook** | 2 min |

### Nonna Says

> Mound your flour on a clean board, make a well deep enough to baptize a baby in, and crack your yolks and whole egg right into the center. Add the spinach puree and a tiny drizzle of olive oil. With a fork, pull flour in from the inner walls, gently, gently, until it thickens into a shaggy mess you can touch with your hands. Now knead, 10 full minutes, pushing with the heel of your palm, folding, turning, until the dough is smooth as silk and springs back when you poke it. Wrap it tight in plastic and let it rest 30 minutes because patience is a virtue and gluten needs to relax. Roll through the pasta machine starting at the widest setting, fold it over itself twice to build structure, then thin it down to the second-to-last setting until you can almost see your hand through it. Cut into tagliatelle, dust with semolina, and boil in very salty water for barely 2 minutes. Toss with browned butter and the extra yolks, or whatever your heart tells you.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → Blanch, ice bath, squeeze bone-dry, puree until smooth. Set aside.**

- 2 cups (60g) fresh spinach *(blanched 30 sec, squeezed dry, pureed smooth)*


**Group B → Pour yolks, egg, spinach puree, oil into flour well. Fork-mix from center outward, then knead 10 min until smooth.**

- 300g (2 1/3 cups) 00 flour *(mounded on board with deep well)*

- 5 egg yolks

- 1 whole egg

- 2 tbsp spinach puree *(from Group A)*

- 1 tsp olive oil *(optional)*

- pinch fine salt


**Group C → Roll rested dough through pasta machine to second-thinnest setting, cut into tagliatelle, dust with semolina.**

-  semolina flour *(for dusting)*

-  heavily salted water *(at a rolling boil)*


**Final assembly:** Wrap dough in plastic, rest 30 min at room temperature → Roll, fold twice for structure, then thin progressively → Cut into tagliatelle, dust with semolina → Boil in heavily salted water for 90 seconds to 2 min → Toss with browned butter, reserved yolks, or your sauce of choice

### Variations

1. **Classic Golden Egg Yolk Pasta** — Skip the spinach and use 7 egg yolks plus 1 whole egg for an intensely rich, golden-yellow pasta with silky chew. ([recipe](https://mosthungry.com/egg-yolk-pasta-dough/))

2. **Squid Ink Pasta** — Add 1 tablespoon squid ink to the egg mixture for dramatic black noodles with a subtle briny flavor, perfect with shellfish. ([recipe](https://www.pestopasteria.com/post/pasta-school-chapter-4-advanced-pasta-dough-techniques))

3. **Saffron Pasta Dough** — Steep a pinch of saffron threads in hot water, cool, then mix into the eggs for golden, aromatic noodles. ([recipe](https://www.the-pasta-project.com/how-to-make-colored-pasta-at-home/))

## Wednesday — Corned Beef & Cabbage with Potatoes and Carrots

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Serious Eats](https://www.seriouseats.com/corned-beef-and-cabbage-recipe) |
| **Servings** | 6-8 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 3.5 hours |

### Nonna Says

> Take that beautiful brisket out of the package, rinse it under cold water to wash off the excess brine, and set it fat-side up in your biggest pot. Cover it with cold water by a couple of inches, toss in the spice packet (or your own peppercorns, mustard seeds, bay leaves, and garlic), and bring it to a boil. Skim the gray foam off the top, because nobody wants to eat that. Drop the heat to a gentle simmer, not a boil, cover the pot, and let it go for about 3 hours until a fork slides in and out like butter. Add your halved potatoes and chunked carrots with 30 minutes to go, and your cabbage wedges with 15 minutes left so they stay tender but not sad. Pull the brisket out, let it rest 10 minutes, then slice it thin against the grain. Pile it on a platter with the vegetables, ladle a little cooking broth over everything, and put the mustard and horseradish on the table where they belong.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → Place brisket in large pot, cover with cold water, add spices. Bring to boil, skim foam, reduce to gentle simmer.**

- 4-5 lb corned beef brisket *(rinsed, fat-side up)*

- 1 spice packet *(included with brisket (or DIY: peppercorns, mustard seed, bay leaves, garlic))*

-  cold water *(enough to cover by 2 inches)*


**Group B → Add to pot after brisket has simmered 2.5 hours. Cook 15 min.**

- 2 lbs small potatoes *(halved or quartered)*

- 1 lb carrots *(peeled, cut into 2-inch pieces)*

- 1 large yellow onion *(cut into wedges)*


**Group C → Add cabbage wedges to pot, cook another 15-20 min until tender.**

- 1 head green cabbage *(cut into 6-8 wedges)*


**Final assembly:** Simmer brisket covered 3 hours total until fork-tender → Add potatoes, carrots, onion at 2.5 hours → Add cabbage at 2 hours 45 min → Rest brisket 10 min, slice thin against the grain → Arrange on platter with vegetables, ladle broth over, serve with mustard and horseradish

### Variations

1. **Instant Pot Corned Beef & Cabbage** — Pressure-cook the brisket for 90 minutes at high pressure for fork-tender results in half the time. ([recipe](https://stripedspatula.com/instant-pot-corned-beef/))

2. **Beer-Braised Oven Corned Beef** — Braise in a Dutch oven at 300F with Guinness stout for 3 hours, then glaze with mustard and brown sugar under the broiler. ([recipe](https://supersafeway.com/the-best-beer-braised-instant-pot-corned-beef-cabbage/))

3. **Slow Cooker Corned Beef** — Layer vegetables on the bottom, brisket on top, set it on low for 8 hours and walk away completely. ([recipe](https://selfproclaimedfoodie.com/pressure-cooker-corned-beef-cabbage/))

## Thursday — Corned Beef Tacos with Pickled Onions & Mustard Crema

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Bon Appetit](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/corned-beef-tacos) |
| **Servings** | 4 (8 tacos) |
| **Prep** | 20 min (plus 30 min pickle time) |
| **Cook** | 10 min |

### Nonna Says

> Start the pickled onions first because they need time to turn pink and gorgeous: slice a red onion thin, heat vinegar, water, sugar, and salt until dissolved, pour it over the onions in a jar, and walk away for 30 minutes. Mix your mustard crema next: sour cream, spicy brown mustard, a squeeze of lime, and if you're feeling brave, a spoonful of horseradish. Now take your leftover corned beef, shred it with two forks or chop it rough, and get it into a hot skillet with a little oil until the edges go crispy and golden, about 5 to 7 minutes. Warm your tortillas in a dry skillet until they puff and get those little brown spots. Load each one up with that crispy beef, a tangle of shredded cabbage, pickled onions, avocado slices, and a generous drizzle of the crema. Finish with cilantro and a squeeze of lime, and try to eat just one.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → Heat vinegar, water, sugar, salt until dissolved. Pour over onion slices, let sit 30 min.**

- 1/2 red onion *(thinly sliced)*

- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar

- 1/2 cup water

- 2 tbsp sugar

- 1 tsp salt


**Group B → Whisk together until smooth. Season with a pinch of salt. Refrigerate.**

- 1/2 cup sour cream or Mexican crema

- 3 tbsp spicy brown mustard

- 1 tbsp lime juice

- 1 tbsp prepared horseradish *(optional)*


**Group C → Heat oil in skillet over medium-high. Cook corned beef until edges are crispy, 5-7 min.**

- 2 cups cooked corned beef *(shredded or chopped (leftover from Wednesday))*

- 1-2 tbsp vegetable oil


**Group 4 → Warm tortillas, assemble with all components.**

- 8 small flour or corn tortillas *(warmed in dry skillet)*

- 1 cup green cabbage *(shredded)*

- 1 avocado *(sliced)*

-  fresh cilantro *(for garnish)*

- 1 lime *(cut into wedges)*


**Final assembly:** Layer crispy corned beef on warm tortillas → Top with shredded cabbage, pickled onions, avocado slices → Drizzle mustard crema generously → Finish with cilantro and a squeeze of lime

### Variations

1. **Reuben Tacos** — Swap pickled onions and crema for sauerkraut, melted Swiss cheese, and Russian dressing for a deli-meets-taqueria mashup. ([recipe](https://40aprons.com/reuben-tacos/))

2. **Korean-Fusion Corned Beef Tacos** — Toss the corned beef with gochujang and sesame oil, top with kimchi slaw and spicy mayo for a Korean street-food spin. ([recipe](https://www.tasteofhome.com/recipes/spicy-corned-beef-tacos/))

3. **Creamy Mustard & Microgreen Tacos** — Add peppery microgreens and a mustard-mayo sauce with a touch of horseradish for a lighter, more refined taco. ([recipe](https://www.runningtothekitchen.com/corned-beef-tacos/))

## Friday — Big Dinner Salad (Vegetarian)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Cookie and Kate](https://cookieandkate.com/big-dinner-salad/) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min (eggs and beets) |

### Nonna Says

> This is not a sad little side salad, this is dinner, so treat it with respect. Drain and rinse your chickpeas, pat them dry, and if you want them extra good, toss them in olive oil with cumin and roast at 400F for 20 minutes until they crackle when you bite them. Boil your eggs for exactly 7 minutes if you want the yolks jammy, then shock them in ice water and peel. Whisk your tahini dressing until it's creamy and pourable: tahini, lemon juice, garlic, a drizzle of olive oil, a touch of maple syrup, and enough water to thin it out. Now build your masterpiece on a big platter: pile up your greens, scatter the chickpeas, arrange the beet wedges and avocado slices, nestle in the halved eggs, and shower it all with the dressing, herbs, and a handful of pepitas. Every bite should have something different happening.

### How It Comes Together

*Preheat oven to 400F (for optional roasted chickpeas)*

**Group A → Whisk tahini, lemon juice, garlic, oil, maple syrup. Add water until pourable. Season with salt.**

- 2 tbsp tahini

- 3 tbsp lemon juice *(about 1 lemon)*

- 1 clove garlic *(minced)*

- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

- 2 tsp maple syrup

- 2-3 tbsp water *(to thin)*

- pinch salt


**Group B → Toss chickpeas with oil, cumin, salt. Roast at 400F for 20 min until crispy (or use straight from the can).**

- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas *(drained, rinsed, patted dry)*

- 1 tbsp olive oil

- 1/2 tsp cumin

- pinch salt


**Group C → Prep eggs, beets, avocado. Arrange greens on a large platter.**

- 2 eggs *(boiled 7 min for jammy yolks, ice-bathed, peeled, halved)*

- 2 small cooked beets *(cut into wedges (pre-cooked or roasted))*

- 1 ripe avocado *(sliced)*

- 4-5 cups mixed greens *(arugula, spinach, romaine)*

- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes *(halved)*

- 2 tbsp fresh herbs *(parsley or dill, chopped)*

- 2 tbsp pepitas or sunflower seeds


**Final assembly:** Spread mixed greens on a large platter or in bowls → Arrange chickpeas, beet wedges, avocado slices, and halved eggs on top → Scatter tomatoes, herbs, and seeds → Drizzle generously with lemon-tahini dressing → Serve immediately while the avocado is perfect

### Variations

1. **Mediterranean Power Bowl** — Add quinoa, feta, olives, and roasted red peppers to transform it into a hearty grain-based power bowl. ([recipe](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/recipes/mediterranean-grain-bowl))

2. **Warm Roasted Vegetable Dinner Salad** — Roast the beets, sweet potato, and chickpeas together, serve warm over greens with a warm lemon-tahini dressing. ([recipe](https://blessthismeal.com/the-best-mediterranean-power-bowls-recipe/))

3. **Vegan Protein Salad with Tofu** — Replace eggs with crispy pan-seared tofu and add marinated white beans for a fully plant-based protein punch. ([recipe](https://www.threelittlechickpeas.com/mediterranean-power-bowl/))

## Saturday — Corned Beef Kimchi Fried Rice Bowls

| | |
|---|---|
| **Source** | [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/263929/corned-beef-kimchi-fried-rice/) |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |

### Nonna Says

> Use yesterday's rice, cold from the fridge, because fresh rice makes fried rice into sad porridge and I will not have it. Drain your kimchi over a bowl and save every drop of that juice because it's liquid gold. Heat oil in a big nonstick skillet, get your chopped corned beef in there and cook it until it starts to crisp on the edges. Chop the kimchi into small pieces, toss it into the pan, and let it all sizzle and get friendly for about 5 minutes. Now dump in the cold rice, the reserved kimchi juice, a splash of water, and a good spoonful of gochujang, then spread it flat across the pan and leave it alone for 3 minutes so the bottom gets crunchy. Stir it up, press it down again, and keep doing that for about 12 minutes until you've got those gorgeous crispy bits throughout. Finish with green onions, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of sesame oil, then crown each bowl with a fried egg with crispy edges and a runny yolk.

### How It Comes Together

**Group A → Heat oil in large nonstick skillet. Add corned beef, cook until edges crisp. Add chopped kimchi, cook 4-5 min until browned.**

- 1 1/4 cups kimchi *(drained, juice reserved, chopped)*

- 2 tbsp olive oil

- 1 1/2 cups cooked corned beef *(diced (leftover from Wednesday))*


**Group B → Add rice, water, kimchi juice, and gochujang to skillet. Spread flat, cook 3-4 min, stir and press. Repeat for 12-15 min total until crusty.**

- 3 cups cold cooked rice *(day-old, from fridge)*

- 1/4 cup water

-  reserved kimchi juice

- 2 tbsp gochujang *(Korean hot pepper paste)*


**Group C → Stir in green onions, sesame seeds, sesame oil, soy sauce. Fry eggs separately in a hot skillet.**

- 1/2 cup green onions *(sliced)*

- 2 tsp toasted sesame seeds

- 2 tsp sesame oil

- 1/4 tsp soy sauce *(to taste)*

- 4 eggs *(fried with crispy edges and runny yolks)*


**Final assembly:** Divide fried rice among 4 bowls → Top each bowl with a fried egg → Garnish with extra green onion and sesame seeds → Serve immediately while the rice is still crackling

### Variations

1. **Spam Kimchi Fried Rice** — Replace corned beef with diced Spam for a Hawaiian-Korean comfort classic with extra salty crunch. ([recipe](https://www.spam.com/recipes/spam-kimchi-fried-rice))

2. **Bacon Kimchi Fried Rice** — Render chopped bacon until crispy, cook everything in the smoky bacon fat for deeply savory flavor. ([recipe](https://onolicioushawaii.com/kimchi-fried-rice/))

3. **Vegan Tofu Kimchi Fried Rice** — Use crispy pan-seared tofu cubes and vegan kimchi, finish with a tofu scramble instead of a fried egg. ([recipe](https://www.downtoearth.org/recipes/appetizers-and-sides/kim-chi-spam-fried-rice))
